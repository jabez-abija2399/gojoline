# Business / company site (consulting, agency, import-export, construction, IT, professional services) — design brief

## Sites opened and what I took
- **stripe.com** — Hero = one outcome sentence ("Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue") + 2 CTAs + logo strip directly under. Proof band of four numbers with labels ("135+ currencies", "$1.9T", "99.999%", "200M+"). Case-study cards = headline + 2 metrics + "Read the story". Closing band "Ready to get started?" with primary + "Contact sales".
- **linear.app** — Announcement pill above the hero, logo strip label ("Powering the companies…"), three numbered pillars, every section the same rhythm: label → bold heading → short paragraph → "Learn more →". Two named quotes, one stat, closing CTA band.
- **ideo.com** — Header button "Say hello". Featured work cards: photo, client logo, outcome-led title ("Better sales, less waste"), one line, "Read more". Testimonials with headshot, name, title. FAQ block near the bottom. CTA "Ask us for references".
- **pentagram.com** — Positioning line "We design everything for everyone" doubling as a filter (discipline × sector). Work cards: image, title, one line, sector tags. Pull quotes between grids.
- **dalberg.com** — Stat line above the headline; proof numbers "25 years · 4,000+ engagements · 148 countries · 60+ nationalities". Region tags on cards (Eastern & Southern Africa). Footprint map tile.
- **kifiya.com** (Addis Ababa) — "Expanding economic opportunities at scale", "since 2012"; order: who we are → impact counters → testimonials (name, role) → partner logo row → "Work with us" form → address, email, phone.
- **skanska.com** (construction) — "Building places that move and inspire." Three offering cards (image, name, 1–2 sentences), project carousel, "Business snapshot" of three numbers, office address in the footer.
- **manypixels construction round-up** — trust first, visible phone number, portfolio prominent, short consultation form, serif heading + sans body, neutral palette so project photos stand out.
- mckinsey.com and macegroup.com refused the fetch (503/403); not used.

## Hero pattern
Text-led, left-aligned: eyebrow (sector + city, "Engineering & construction · Addis Ababa · Since 2009"), a value proposition in 6–10 words stating the outcome for the client, one supporting sentence, primary button "Request a quote / Talk to us" and secondary "Our projects". A wide photo (team, site or city) sits to the right or below, never under the text without a dark overlay. Proof numbers or a client strip finish the first screen.

## Home page order
1. Header: logo, Services, Projects, About, Contact; phone number; primary button
2. Hero
3. Proof band: 3–4 numbers (years, projects delivered, clients, staff/cities)
4. Services: 3–6 cards with icon, name, 1–2 lines, link
5. Sectors / clients: logo row or plain sector chips
6. Projects / case studies: photo, client or location, sector tag, one result line
7. Process: 3–4 numbered steps
8. Why us / about: photo + short paragraph + certifications, licences
9. Team: leadership headshots, name, role
10. Testimonial: one quote, name, title, company
11. Enquiry band: short form (name, phone, service, message) + phone, email, address, map
12. Footer: services, company, registration/TIN, socials

## Signature UI
- Big numbers with small labels
- Icon service cards on a 2×3 grid
- Project cards with a location/sector tag and one metric
- Numbered process steps joined by a line
- Logo strip in greyscale
- Enquiry form with a service dropdown; phone in the header

## Typography
Confident sans for everything (Inter, Manrope, IBM Plex Sans, Geist), headings 600–700 weight, slightly tight tracking, sentence case. Law/finance/consulting can use a serif display (Source Serif, Fraunces) over sans body. Eyebrow labels 12px uppercase +0.08em. Numbers 40–56px, tabular. Hero 34–40px at 360px, 56–72px desktop. Amharic: no uppercase or tracking tricks; weight contrast only.

## Palettes
- **Deep navy + amber (construction, trade, engineering)**: primary `#1D4ED8`, dark accent `#0B1B33`, light background `#F5F7FA`, text `#111827` (highlight amber `#F59E0B` for numbers only)
- **Forest + gold (consulting, finance, agri/import-export)**: primary `#0F5B46`, dark accent `#0B2E26`, light background `#F7F5EF`, text `#17211D` (gold `#C8962E` as thin accent)

## Photo style
Real work in progress: site with crane, warehouse aisle, team round a table mid-discussion, handshake outdoors, Addis skyline at dusk. Wide 3:2 crops, natural light, people not looking at the camera. Office interiors empty and clean for About. Avoid cliché close-ups unless used small.

## What converts on mobile
- Tap-to-call and WhatsApp/Telegram buttons, sticky at the bottom
- Proof numbers in a 2×2 grid inside the first two screens
- Enquiry form of 3–4 fields with a service picker; no long RFP forms
- Services as a stacked list that opens, project cards one per row
- Address + map link + opening hours near the form

## Do
1. State what the company does, for whom and where in one sentence.
2. Put 3–4 honest numbers directly under the hero.
3. Give every project a location, sector and result.
4. Repeat one primary action (quote/call) after every second section.
5. Show licences, certifications and named clients — trust is the product.

## Don't
1. No vague mission slogans ("Excellence through innovation") as the headline.
2. No stock handshake as the hero or auto-rotating sliders.
3. No walls of text for services — 2 lines each, details on a second level.
