# Construction niche brief (building materials supplier / hardware store / small contractor, finishing and renovation)

Method note: pages were fetched as text on 2026-11-18, so structure, copy, card fields, units and CTAs below are observed; typeface and colour statements are niche conventions and my own recommendation, not measured from the CSS. Ethiopian firms in this trade rarely have their own sites: most live on the 2merkato.com directory and Telegram, so the Ethiopian evidence is directory listings plus news reporting on how prices are quoted.

## Sites opened and what I took from each
- **selcobw.com (UK builders' merchant)**: promo strip first ("tradeREWARDS is now live! Silver members save 6%, Gold members save 10%"); header has "Branches" and "Get a Trade Card"; 11 categories, each with sub-links and "View all..."; deal tiles print the unit right after the price ("£3.75each"); delivery stated as a rule: "FREE Delivery on orders over £150 ex VAT"; phone ordering for account holders ("Dial & Deliver… call us on 03331 886 388"). Positioning line: "it's where the trade go! We stock all the building materials… under one roof."
- **jewson.co.uk (UK merchant)**: a "VAT EX / INC" toggle in the header (business buyers think ex-VAT); "Project Estimator" in the top nav; "Popular categories" row (Aggregate, Fencing, Insulation, Plasterboard…); units live in the product name ("Postfix Plastic Bag 20kg"); delivery-or-collect chosen by location pop-up; "Open an account… we have an account for everyone." Weak: product cards say "See price" instead of a price.
- **cashbuild.co.za (South African mass-market hardware)**: header button "Request A Quote" plus "My Quotes"; store selector ("Upington | Change Store"); nine big category tiles in the trade's own words (Water Tanks, Roofing, Bricks, Cement, Steel, Aggregates…); "Building Material Calculator" and "Bill of Materials" tools; price cards with units inside the name ("6m", "5L"). Weak: image-only hero slider with no readable headline.
- **mcmaster.com (industrial supplier)**: function over decoration: one "Choose a Category" list of 29 categories with every sub-category visible as plain links; service blurbs as promises ("Same or next day delivery", "Here when you need us"); phone number in the header. The lesson is a dense, scannable text list loads fast and is trusted.
- **mabati.com (Kenyan roofing manufacturer)**: top bar "Talk to us" with phone, buttons BUY NOW / WARRANTY / DESIGN MY HOME; products grouped residential vs industrial/commercial; "Where to buy" with retail centres, authorised distributors and an installer finder. No prices or units anywhere, which leaves the buyer to phone for everything.
- **victorianplumbing.co.uk/tiles (tile retailer)**: every tile card priced per square metre ("£34.95/m²", tile size in the name "65 x 250mm"); accessories priced per item ("£25.95" for 20kg adhesive); "Was £19.95/m²" + "SAVE 25%"; "Order up to 3 free tile samples"; FAQ tells you to measure height x width and use the tile calculator.
- **blockrenovation.com (US renovation platform)**: "Renovations Built On Trust"; four numbered steps right after the hero ("Tell us your renovation needs" → "Get contractor matches" → "Hire with confidence" → "Stay in control"); proof as percentages ("88% of projects stay within 5% of the original proposal", "90% completed on schedule"); "Workmanship Warranty", contractor "verification of licensing and insurance". Weak: no gallery on the home page.
- **2merkato.com — Safecon General Construction PLC (Addis)**: bilingual name "ሴፍኮን ጠቅላላ ስራ ተቋራጭ ኃ.የተ.የግ.ማ"; Amharic services heading "የምንሰጣቸው አገልግሎቶች"; services in plain trade words (waterproofing, concrete and formwork, ceramic tiling, painting, sanitary, electrical); named developers it subcontracted for; states a goal "to rise to Grade 1"; address down to sub-city and woreda (Kolfe Keraniyo, Woreda 03); two mobile numbers in both 09… and +251 forms.
- **2merkato.com — Procon Interior Construction and Fit-out (Bole)**: proof line "35+ Projects completed, 5+ Years in service"; six named clients (PVH, ONTEX, Dashen, Girl Effect, EBC…); 24 project photos. Weak: no grade or licence number anywhere.
- **2merkato.com — Gojo construction materials and Magemet Trading (Addis)**: product lists (gypsum board "local and imported", cement boards, metal framing, waterproof membrane) but no units, prices, hours or delivery; Magemet shows 31 uncaptioned photos. - **t.me/s/constructionproxy (Ethiopian Telegram channel)**: Amharic headline, English body ("Check the Latest Construction Material Prices for 01 February 2025"); estimate tables use the columns "Item Description · Unit · Direct Rate". ## Hero pattern
Text-led on a solid dark band, not a slider. Eyebrow: trade + area ("Cement, steel & finishing · Merkato, Addis Ababa"). Headline 5–8 words naming what you sell or build ("Cement, rebar and tiles, delivered to your site"; contractor: "Finishing and renovation you can inspect"). One line of proof ("Since 2012 · Delivery across Addis · Grade 5 BC"). Two buttons: "See today's prices" + "Request a quote" (contractor: "Request a quote" + "See our projects"). One real photo of the yard, stock or a finished room to the side or below. A small dated line under the buttons: "Prices updated 18 Nov 2026".

## Home page order (top to bottom)
Supplier / hardware:
1. Header: logo, Prices, Delivery, About, Contact, phone button.
2. Hero.
3. Price list (the core of the site): grouped by category, each row = item, spec, unit, price, updated date.
4. Category tiles: Cement, Steel & rebar, Aluminium & profiles, Tiles & ceramics, Gypsum & ceiling, Paint, Sanitary, Tools.
5. Delivery band: areas served as chips, minimum order, truck sizes, same-day cut-off.
6. Business customers: contractor/bulk pricing, credit on agreement, proforma for tenders.
7. Quote request form.
8. Brands / factories stocked (Dangote, Derba, Mugher…) as text or greyscale logos.
9. Address, map link, hours, Telegram. Footer with TIN and trade licence number.

Contractor / finishing:
1. Header with phone. 2. Hero. 3. Proof band: grade, years, projects done, staff. 4. Services list (6 max). 5. Project gallery with before/after. 6. Process steps (visit → quote → work → handover). 7. Named clients. 8. Licence & warranty block. 9. Quote form + contact.

## Signature UI
- **Price list table**: rows "Dangote OPC 42.5 · per quintal · ETB —", "Rebar Ø12 mm, 12 m · per piece / per kg", "Ceramic floor tile 60x60 · per m²", "Corrugated sheet G32 · per piece", "Aluminium profile · per bar (6 m)". Unit always in its own column, price right-aligned in tabular numerals, "Call for price" allowed, date stamp on the section. Never prices with decimals in floats; store minor units.
- **Unit chip** on every product card (per quintal / per piece / per m² / per kg / per bar / per litre).
- **Quick calculator** for tiles: area m² → boxes needed (+10% waste); cement: bags for a slab is optional later.
- **Quote request form**: name, phone, company (optional), items as a repeatable "item + quantity + unit" row or a free text box, delivery area (select), needed by (date), "Need a proforma invoice" checkbox. Sends to Telegram.
- **Delivery area chips** (Bole, Kality, Ayat, Lebu, Summit, outside Addis on request) with a fee line.
- **Credentials block** for contractors: grade badge "GC-3" / "BC-5" with licence number, issuing body and expiry, TIN, VAT registered; warranty line ("12-month workmanship warranty on finishing").
- **Project card**: photo (before/after slider or two images side by side), project type, location, size ("320 m² office fit-out, Bole"), duration, client name if allowed.
- **Business customer strip**: "Contractors and developers: ask for volume prices and credit terms."

## Typography
Industrial and plain: a sturdy grotesque or condensed sans for headings (Barlow / Barlow Semi Condensed / IBM Plex Sans class), weight 600–700, body Inter or Noto Sans 16 px. Tabular numerals in every price and quantity. Section labels 12 px uppercase +0.06em in English only. Hero 32–36 px at 360 px, 52–64 px desktop. Amharic: Noto Sans Ethiopic, weight contrast only; check "m²", "Ø12 mm" and "ኩንታል" sit on one line in the unit column.

## Palettes
- **Safety Yellow** (supplier, hardware): primary charcoal `#1F2328`, accent yellow `#F2B705` (buttons on dark, highlights), background `#F6F5F2`, text `#16181B`; price green `#1E7B4F` for "in stock".
- **Concrete & Terracotta** (contractor, finishing): primary terracotta `#B5502F`, dark accent `#2B2622`, background `#F4F1EC`, text `#1D1A17`; neutral concrete grey `#8A8580` for tags.
Yellow text never on white; use it as a fill behind dark text.

## Photo style
Real stock and real sites: stacked cement bags, rebar bundles, tile showroom wall, loaded Isuzu truck at the gate, a finished bathroom, gypsum ceiling, a crew at work in PPE. Daylight, straight horizontals, 4:3 crops, compressed to under 120 KB each. Before/after pairs taken from the same spot. No glossy render passed off as a finished job; label "Design render" when used.

## What converts on mobile
- Sticky bottom bar: Call, Telegram, "Get a quote".
- Price list as a collapsible list per category with the price visible without tapping; a search field above it once there are more than 30 items.
- Quote form of 4–5 fields with free-text items; "Send list on Telegram" as an alternative (people photograph their bill of quantities).
- Delivery areas and hours near the form; tap-to-map.
- The "updated" date on prices: in a market that moves weekly, an old price is worse than none.

## Do
1. Show a unit next to every price, in the units the trade uses (quintal, piece, kg, m², bar).
2. Date the price list and let the owner update one row from the dashboard or chat.
3. State delivery areas, minimum order and fee in one block.
4. Contractors: show grade, licence number and named projects with location and size.
5. Offer a business/bulk line with proforma invoice for tenders.

## Don't
1. No image-only sliders or "See price" placeholders; a real number or "Call for price".
2. No online card checkout or cart; ordering is a quote/enquiry to Telegram, payment in Birr on delivery or by transfer.
3. No uncaptioned photo walls: every project image needs type, place and size.
