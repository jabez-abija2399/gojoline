# Worship (church) template brief

Method note: pages were read with a text fetcher, so section order, copy and button labels are observed; typography, colour and photo remarks are design judgement and recommendation, not measured from the pages.

## Sites opened and what each gave

- **elevationchurch.org**: hero "Welcome to Elevation Church / See what God can do through you" with two buttons ("Get connected", "Learn more"); then latest sermon banner ("Watch sermon"); "Find the right experience for you" (4 cards: campus, live stream...); promo cards; "A place for you and your family" (7 ministry cards, each one line + "Learn more"); "How to get involved" (Volunteer / Lead). "Give" is the first nav item.
- **hillsong.com**: hero "Hillsong Church / Welcome Home" plus a two-sentence belief statement; "Find a church" is the dominant action; campus cards carry practical badges ("Free Parking", "Wheelchair Accessible", "Interpretation Available", "Parents Room"). Closes with a one-line mission and repeated CTA.
- **redeemer.com** (page only partly returned): persistent "Give" button in the header; congregations listed by neighbourhood; ministries as a plain list; feature cards dated like news. Restrained, text-led.
- **stmaryeotc.org** (Debre Zihon St Mary, Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo): opens with the Trinitarian invocation in Ge'ez/Amharic then English ("በስመ አብ ወወልድ ወመንፈስ ቅዱስ..."); nav has Services (Worship & prayer, Baptism, Holy Wedding, Funeral & Memorial Prayer), Sunday School, Teachings/ትምህርቶች, Fast & Feast calendar, Media (Mezmur, sermons), Clergy. Giving is split into named purposes (tithe/መባ, covenant gift, icon framing), each with its own button. Weekly schedule + calendar. Weak visually (unlabelled slideshow, no hero action): the content model is right, the presentation is the opportunity.
- **saintsophiadc.org** (Greek Orthodox cathedral): "Welcome" + history; **"Worship Schedule"** block directly under the hero (Orthros 8:30, Divine Liturgy 9:30); liturgical calendar with next three feast services; daily meditation; "WE WELCOME ALL OUR VISITORS"; virtual candle; sacrament certificate requests; address/phone in footer. Overloaded with tiles lower down: a warning.
- Failed to load: htb.org (403), stpaulsauckland.org.nz (503). Webflow template gallery returned a parse error; Squarespace/Wix galleries were not opened. One roundup search (Hostinger, REACHRIGHT, Givebutter lists) confirmed only general points: design around the first-time guest's next step.

## Shared patterns

**Hero**: one full-bleed photo (building exterior or gathered people), a 2 to 5 word welcome, one sentence of identity, and at most two buttons. Best pair for Ethiopia: primary "Service times" (scrolls to block) or "Plan your visit"; secondary "Watch / Listen" or "Get directions". Orthodox parishes: put the invocation as a small line above the headline.

**Home page order (recommended)**
1. Header: name (Amharic + English), language switch, "Give" button.
2. Hero.
3. Service times strip: 2 to 4 rows (day, service name, time), address, "Get directions". For Orthodox: Kidase (ቅዳሴ), Sunday school, evening prayer; for Protestant: Sunday worship, Bible study, prayer night, youth.
4. Welcome / about: 2 short paragraphs + photo of clergy or congregation; "What to expect" bullets (language of service, dress, children).
5. Ministries / services cards (3 to 6): Sunday school, choir/mezmur, youth, women, charity; or sacraments (baptism, wedding, memorial).
6. Latest teaching: one sermon/mezmur card with title, speaker, date, play link (YouTube/Telegram).
7. Upcoming events / feast and fast calendar: next 3 dated items.
8. Giving: short verse, named funds, bank/Telebirr details with copy button.
9. Gallery (4 to 6 photos).
10. Contact: map, phone, Telegram/YouTube/Facebook, office hours.

**Signature elements**: service-times block; "next feast/event" date chips (large day number + month); sermon card with play icon; ministry cards; scripture verse as a quiet divider; named giving funds; clergy/pastor portrait with name and title.

**Typography**: serif display for headlines (liturgical, dignified) with a clean sans for body and times; Orthodox sites suit sentence-case serif headlines at 32 to 40px mobile; evangelical sites suit bolder sans headlines, sometimes uppercase eyebrow labels (12px, letter-spaced). Amharic: test with Noto Serif Ethiopic / Noto Sans Ethiopic, line-height 1.6+, never all-caps tracking on Ethiopic.

**Palettes**
- A "Tewahedo" (Orthodox/Catholic): primary `#8B1E2D` crimson, dark accent `#2A1215`, light background `#FAF5EA` parchment, text `#231A17`; optional gold rule `#C8962E`.
- B "Highland morning" (Protestant/Evangelical): primary `#1D4E6B` lake blue, dark accent `#0F2A3A`, light background `#F6F3EE`, text `#1B2430`; optional warm accent `#E0A33A`.

**Photo style**: real building first (rock-hewn church, domes, cross), then people in white netela seen in groups, clergy with cross/candle/drum, choir, hands on a Bible. Warm, low-saturation, natural light. Heroes need a dark or plain area for text; add a 40 to 55% dark gradient. Cards crop 4:3 on a detail (cross, drum, book).

**What converts on mobile**: times and address visible within one scroll; tap-to-call and "Get directions" as full-width buttons; sticky bottom bar with "Times" and "Give"; sermon opens in YouTube/Telegram instead of an embedded player (slow connections); giving shows account numbers as copyable text, not only a card form.

## Do
1. Put service times and location directly under the hero.
2. Limit the hero to two buttons with plain verbs.
3. Show the next three dated events/feasts, not a full calendar grid.
4. Name where gifts go (tithe, building, charity) with local payment details.
5. Write every label in Amharic and English; let the invocation or a verse lead on Orthodox sites.

## Don't
1. No image sliders with unlabelled slides or text baked into images.
2. No wall of promo tiles (Saint Sophia has 20+): cap cards at six per section.
3. No autoplay video or heavy embedded players on the home page.
