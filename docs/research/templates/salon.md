# Salon / barber / spa — design brief

## Sites opened and what I took
- **heritagebarbershop.com** — Hero: full-width photo + "SOUTHEAST PORTLAND'S CLASSIC BARBERSHOP EXPERIENCE" + "BOOK NOW". One-page order: About (hours "Open Daily 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM", phone, address, "Open in Google Maps") → Services price list (name → short description → price; "$60+", "$70 / $90" variants, discount row, footnote) → Barbers cards (portrait, name, working days, "Book Now >") → Reviews (3 quotes, Google/Yelp) → Gift cards.
- **birdsbarbershop.com** — Hero line "Haircuts for all y'all" + "Book Now". Three value props with hand-drawn icons, award badge, "We keep it simple" service cards with photos (Short cut, Skin fade, Kid cut…), named testimonials, location tiles, Instagram feed, closing CTA "Get a great haircut. Have a great time."
- **blindbarber.com** — "Book Appointment" always in the header; black logo, small all-caps labels, press quotes row, founder's note as a blockquote. Product-led and less useful for a single salon.
- **fellowbarber.com** — Dark "Book Now" button in the header, press strip (GQ, Esquire), "Stop by and see us" + Locations.
- **fresha.com barbershop listing** — the booking-row standard: service name / "From $45" / "30 min – 45 min"; three services previewed then "See all N services"; rating + review count beside the name; pick service → professional → time.
- **glossier.com** — beauty tone: short, playful sentence-case copy, uppercase section headings, status badges ("Best Seller", "New"), brand line "Skin First. Makeup Second".
- **nanoglobals review of 20 barbershop sites** and **wix salon examples** — keep booking on-site; show price *and* duration for every service; a profile per stylist ("customers book people, not time slots"); embed reviews; photograph the real space; palettes praised: monochrome black/white, soft neutrals with white space, earth tones.
- Squarespace beauty pages returned only category links (no template detail); heritagebarbershoppdx.com did not resolve.

## Hero pattern
Full-bleed or large rounded photo of real work (braids, fade, finished style) with a dark gradient; short headline naming the place and the craft ("Braids, cuts and colour in Bole"); one line with area + today's hours ("Open today 8:00–20:00"); primary button "Book now" (WhatsApp/Telegram/phone) and secondary "See prices". Optional rating chip ("4.8 ★ · 320 reviews").

## Home page order
1. Header: logo, Services, Team, Gallery, Contact; "Book" button always visible
2. Hero
3. Quick facts strip: hours today, area, phone
4. Services & prices by category (Hair, Braids, Barber, Nails, Spa) — rows with name, duration, price
5. Gallery: finished looks, before/after pairs
6. Stylists: portrait, name, speciality, working days
7. Reviews: 3 short quotes with names
8. About the salon: interior photo + 2 paragraphs
9. Visit: opening hours table, address, map link, phone
10. Footer: socials (Instagram, TikTok, Telegram)

## Signature UI
- Price list rows with dotted leaders: `Box braids · 3 h ······ from 1,500 Br`
- Category tabs or pills above the list
- "from" prices and duration chips
- Stylist cards with round or 4:5 portraits
- Before/after pair or slider
- Opening-hours table with today highlighted and an "Open now" dot
- Sticky bottom bar: Book · Call · Directions

## Typography
Beauty salon: elegant high-contrast serif for headings (Playfair Display, Cormorant, Fraunces) + clean sans body; generous spacing. Barber: bold condensed or slab sans in uppercase with wide tracking (Oswald, Archivo, Bebas-like) + sans body. Labels 11–12px uppercase +0.1em. Prices in tabular figures. Hero 36–44px at 360px. Amharic: skip uppercase and condensed faces; use weight and size.

## Palettes
- **Clay rose + espresso (beauty salon / spa)**: primary `#B4533C`, dark accent `#2A1A14`, light background `#FBF4EE`, text `#2B211C`
- **Black + brass (barber / unisex)**: primary `#B8872B`, dark accent `#111111`, light background `#F6F2EA`, text `#1A1A1A`

## Photo style
Close, warm, skin-true: hands braiding, the back of a finished style, a fade in profile, natural hair portraits on plain backdrops. Vertical 4:5 crops for looks, wide 3:2 for the interior (mirrors, chairs, warm light). Black-and-white works for barber mood. Products only unbranded. No stiff posed group shots.

## What converts on mobile
- "Book" in a sticky bottom bar on every scroll position; tap opens WhatsApp/Telegram/call
- Prices visible without a tap or a PDF; durations beside them
- Today's hours and "Open now" in the first screen
- Map link that opens the maps app; phone as a button
- Gallery as a swipeable row of vertical photos

## Do
1. Publish every price with a duration; use "from" where it varies.
2. Group services into 3–5 categories with pills.
3. Put a Book button in the header, hero, after prices and in a sticky bar.
4. Show named stylists with their speciality and days.
5. Use real finished-look photos and 3 named reviews.

## Don't
1. Don't hide prices behind "DM for price" or an image of a menu.
2. Don't send booking to a dead-end third-party page or a long form.
3. Don't use pale grey text on photos or script fonts for prices — they fail on small screens.
