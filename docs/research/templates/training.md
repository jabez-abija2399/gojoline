# Training centre site (tutoring, national-exam prep, IELTS/TOEFL, language school, computer/coding, driving school) — design brief

Scope note: `school.md` covers schools and kindergartens (age/grade cards, crest, director's welcome, term dates). This template is for paid short courses sold to adults, students and parents: the course card with duration, fee and next intake is the product, the timetable answers "can I fit this in?", and registration is the action. Pages were fetched as text; structure, copy, numbers and fields are observed, typefaces and colours are my recommendation.

## Sites opened and what I took
- **mizantechinstitute.com** (Addis, computer/coding) — The closest model. Hero "Mizan Institute of Technology for future-ready careers", buttons "Register for the 14th round" + "Explore all courses", stat row "20+ courses · 4 attendance modes · 14th round · 500 ETB one-time registration fee". Course cards = title, one line, duration, in-person / online fee (Full Stack MERN 6 months 6,000 / 5,000 ETB; Graphic Design 3 months 5,000 / 4,000 ETB; AI 3 months 7,500 / 7,000 ETB). Separate schedule block: Regular Mon/Wed/Fri or Tue/Thu/Sat 9:00–11:00 or 1:30–3:30; Extension 5:00–7:00 pm; Weekend Sat–Sun 9:00–12:00. Registration in 4 steps (details → course → mode → upload ID, photo, payment receipt), reviewed in 1–2 business days. Discounts: 10% full payment, 7% for 2–3 courses. Instructors with photo + course. "Verify certificate" in the main nav: each certificate has an ID ("MiT-CERT-2026-…") and QR. FAQ (registration fee, live vs recorded, switching modes). Weak: no next-intake date per course, empty "Student voices", Telegram only as a channel link.
- **lewagon.com** (coding bootcamp) — Badge above the hero ("1st-ranked bootcamp… 6,000+ reviews"), headline "Build your future. Learn Tech & AI.", CTAs "Contact an advisor" + "Find your program". Program cards = title, hours (400h / 200h / 40h), one line, "Learn more". Ratings band 4.98/5 with source logos, "33,000 alumni". "See tuition and financing" in the header; "Next sessions" is its own page. Lesson: hours on the card, two course families (long programme vs short skill course).
- **e2language.com** (IELTS/PTE/OET prep online) — Hero "Online English Exam Preparation" + "We've prepared over 2,722,270 students". Exam picker: "Which test are you preparing for?" with 9 test cards. Result testimonials = stars, quote, country, test taken, and the actual scores (Speaking 90/90, Writing 80/90…). Lesson: pick the exam first; show scores, not adjectives.
- **ieltsadvantage.com** — Hero speaks to the pain: "Can't Get The IELTS Score You Need?". Proof bar "12+ years · 10,000+ Band 7, 8 & 9 results". "Does this sound like you?" list, method in 4 steps (Watch, Follow, Submit, Succeed), guarantees block, teachers with years taught ("former examiner, 21+ years"), a cheap first step ("$10 diagnostic test"), disclaimer "not affiliated with the British Council, IDP or Cambridge". Lesson: a low-cost level test as the entry point; honest non-affiliation line.
- **bsm.co.uk** (driving school, UK) — Hero "Learn to drive / Discover freedom", single CTA "Get Prices". Three value points (quality instructors, lessons that fit around you, book online), "professionally trained and DBS checked" instructors, 4 steps: licence → book a lesson → theory test → pass. Lesson: the licence journey as numbered steps is the page.
- **ef.com** (language school) — Programmes split by audience (Kids & teens 12–16 / 16–18, Adults 18–25 / 25+ / 50+, Companies), languages listed as chips, "Free brochure" with prices and start dates, level test (EF SET) as a resource. Weak: no durations or prices on the home page.
- **temari.pages.dev** (Addis tutoring prototype) — "Learn from a tutor you can trust", grades 1–12, online / hybrid / home. Tutor search with sort (Top rated, Most sessions, Lowest price). Tutor profile fields: photo, name, grades, subjects, location, languages, availability, education, experience, price per 8 or 12 sessions a month. Materials "200 birr a month, or free with a tutor". Says plainly it is a prototype with sample tutors.
- **enattutor.vercel.app** (Addis home tutoring) — "AAU Graduate Tutors", "a mother's care", four highlights (Grades 1–12, Flexible hours, Online & in-person, Addis Ababa), two packages (National curriculum / IGCSE & IB) priced hourly with "Call for pricing", free assessment, phone + Telegram. Weak: no tutor faces, no prices, two different phone numbers.
- **t.me/s/fideltutorial** (Fidel Tutorial, Telegram channel) — Posts mostly in Amharic: home tutors, after-school coding/music/sport at Gerji and Tafo, national-exam help, free webinar with date in both calendars (21 Tikimt / Oct 31), registration through a Google Form link, two phone numbers. Lesson: this is how Ethiopian centres already sell; the site must be at least as quick as a Telegram post.
- **smuc.edu.et/testing-center** (St. Mary's University, TOEFL/GRE test centre) — Register 3–4 months ahead, regular registration closes 7 days before, late 3 days with an extra fee; arrive 30 minutes early with ID; directions by landmark ("behind the Ethiopian Petroleum Enterprise, about 300 m below Federal Police HQ, Mexico Square"). Lesson: deadlines and landmark directions; separate "we prepare you" from "official test centre".
- **expat.com forum — driving licence in Addis** — Register at a driving school (about 2 weeks), written theory exam (in Amharic), about 25 days of field training, field exam. No school named and no costs given anywhere. Lesson: a driving school that publishes steps, days and fee wins on clarity alone.
- Refused or unreachable: ethiopia.britishcouncil.org (503), goethe.de Addis course page and kumon.com (403), the "Texas International Study Center" Addis page (host not reachable from this session). Not used.

## Hero pattern
Text-led, left-aligned on a plain or lightly tinted background, with a real classroom/lab/car photo beside or below (never behind the text). Eyebrow with the intake: "Registration open · next intake 2 Tahsas 2019 E.C. (11 Dec)". Headline names the outcome in 5–9 words ("Pass IELTS with the band you need", "Get your driving licence in 3 months", "Learn to code. Get hired."). One line: courses + mode + place ("Day, evening and weekend classes in Bole, or live online"). Primary "Register now", secondary "See courses & fees". Under the buttons, 3 facts in a row: number of courses · fee from · registration fee.

## Home page order
1. Notice bar: next intake date + days left + phone
2. Header: logo, Courses, Timetable, Results, Teachers, FAQ; "Register" button; አማ / EN
3. Hero
4. Course finder chips (Exam prep · Languages · Computer & coding · Driving · Tutoring) or, for exam prep, "Which exam?" cards (Grade 12 national exam, IELTS, TOEFL, Duolingo, SAT)
5. Course cards (see spec below)
6. Timetable: shift table (Regular, Evening/Extension, Weekend, Online)
7. Results: pass rate, band/score wall, graduates' quotes, certificate verify
8. How to register: 4 numbered steps ending in the form
9. Teachers: photo, name, subject, qualification, years teaching
10. Accreditation and trust: licence from the relevant authority, partner logos, honest non-affiliation line for exam brands
11. Registration form (+ payment instructions)
12. FAQ (6–8)
13. Contact: phone, Telegram, landmark directions, map link, office hours. Footer with licence number and report-abuse link

## Course card spec
Title · level/who for (Beginner, Grade 11–12, IELTS Academic) · duration (weeks or hours, number of sessions) · schedule shift chips (Mon/Wed/Fri · 5–7 pm) · mode (In person / Online / Both) · fee in Birr, integer, "ETB 5,000" with in-person / online split if different, "or 2 × ETB 2,500" when paid by month · next intake date with seats left ("12 seats left") · certificate yes/no · "Register" (pre-fills form) and "Details". Driving: licence category (Automobile, Public transport, Dry cargo…), theory hours, practical days, fee including/excluding the official test fee.

## Signature UI
- Course cards with a big tabular fee and a coloured "next intake" date block
- Shift timetable as a 4-row table that scrolls inside its own box on mobile, never the page
- Score wall: student first name + initial, exam, score chip ("IELTS 7.5", "Grade 12: 512/600"), year
- Certificate check: one input "Enter certificate number" → valid / not found
- Dual dates (E.C. and G.C.) on every intake and deadline
- Countdown "9 days to intake" in the notice bar (computed, no animation)
- Teacher cards with qualification line and subject chips

## Typography
Readable over decorative: Lexend or Atkinson Hyperlegible for headings and body (designed for reading ease), 600–700 headings, 16–17px body, line height 1.6. Fees and scores in tabular figures 24–32px. Eyebrow labels 12px uppercase +0.06em. Hero 30–36px at 360px, 52–60px desktop. Different from `school.md`: no serif heritage headings, no crest; this is a practical, modern product page. Amharic: Noto Sans Ethiopic for everything, weights 400/700 only, +15% line height, no uppercase; course names often stay in English ("IELTS", "Python"), so mix scripts within one line cleanly.

## Palettes
- **Indigo & lime (exam prep, coding, computer)**: primary `#3730A3`, dark accent `#1E1B4B`, light background `#F7F7FB`, text `#18181B` (lime highlight `#A3E635` for intake blocks and score chips, always with dark text)
- **Teal & signal orange (languages, tutoring, driving)**: primary `#0F766E`, dark accent `#134E4A`, light background `#F6FAF9`, text `#1C2524` (orange `#EA580C` for "seats left" and deadlines only)

## Photo style
Real classes at the real centre: students at computers with screens visible, a teacher at the whiteboard mid-explanation, small speaking-practice groups, a learner driver with the instructor in the car, graduation day holding certificates. Daylight, eye level, faces engaged, 3:2 crops. No stock "students in a lecture hall abroad", no exam-brand logos used as decoration.

## Registration and payment
Form: full name · phone (required) · Telegram username (optional) · course (pre-filled) · shift · mode · level/current grade or target score · how did you hear · note. Second step shows payment: registration fee + first instalment by Telebirr or bank (account name and number as text, copy buttons), then "Send receipt" (photo upload or send to Telegram). The submission goes to the owner's Telegram with all fields; the student sees "We will confirm your seat by phone within 1 working day". No card checkout. Free level test or first trial class as a softer second CTA.

## What converts on mobile
- Sticky bottom bar: "Register" + Telegram (call as icon)
- Fee and next intake visible on every course card without opening it
- Exam/course chips that filter in place, no page reload
- Form of 4 required fields max; receipt upload optional at first step
- Landmark directions ("2nd floor, above Dashen Bank, near Bole Medhanialem") + office hours next to the phone

## Do
1. Put duration, fee in Birr and next intake on every course card.
2. Publish the timetable shifts; most learners work or study during the day.
3. Show results with numbers (pass rate, scores, number of graduates) and a certificate check.
4. Give dates in both Ethiopian and Gregorian calendars.
5. State accreditation honestly, and say you are not the official IELTS/TOEFL test centre if you are not.

## Don't
1. No "Call for price": hidden fees are the first thing students complain about.
2. No guaranteed-pass or guaranteed-visa claims, no fake score screenshots.
3. No long course syllabus on the home page: 3 outcome bullets, details on the course page.
4. No portal logins, chat widgets or auto-playing promo videos on the public site.
