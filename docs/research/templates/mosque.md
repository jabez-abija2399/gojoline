# Mosque / Islamic centre template brief

Method note: pages were read with a text fetcher, so section order, copy and labels are observed; typography, colour and photo remarks are design judgement and recommendation, not measured from the pages.

## Sites opened and what each gave

- **cambridgecentralmosque.org**: the strongest model. Header shows Gregorian + Hijri date ("16th November 2026 · 6 Jamada Al-Thani 1448"), a "Donate" button and two pills: "JUM'A 12:15 & 13:00" and "PRAYER TIMES". Prayer strip: FAJR, ZUHR, ASR, MAGHRIB, ISHA with two rows (begins / jama'at). Hero is one line about the building. Then three intro cards (About us, Plan Your Visit, Support Your Mosque); "Visit the Mosque" (opening hours, guided tours); events; **Services** as six cards each with its own action: Shahadas ("Learn more and book"), Nikahs, Madrasa, Meet an Imam, Janazas ("Make arrangements here"), General Events; Friday sermon; sustainability story; footer with address and charity number.
- **eastlondonmosque.org.uk**: prayer row directly under the hero with "Begins" and "Jamā'ah" sub-columns for the five prayers; **Quick Donate** widget with a hadith line, £30/£50/£100/Other and "Set up a regular payment"; then schools, counselling, hall hire, new Muslim support, news. Services live in the menu (Prayers, Education, Birth, marriage & death, women's services, youth hub).
- **greenlanemasjid.org**: "Daily Prayer Times" panel with live clock, table Fajr / Sunrise / Dhuhr / Asr / Maghrib / Isha in "Start" and "Jamat" columns, link to PDF timetable; separate **"Jumu'ah Khutbah"** line naming the khateeb, language and time; "Support Your Masjid Today" backed by numbers (5,000+ weekly congregants, 700 daily iftar meals); news cards; bank details printed in the footer. Tagline "Inspire | Educate | Serve".
- **mcec.org.uk** (Palmers Green Mosque, small community mosque): hero "Welcome to MCEC!" + one line + "Learn More"; table Namaz / Time / Jamaat including a **Jum'ah row**; cards Prayers, Venue Hire, Weddings, Funerals, Guided Tours; events list (Quran classes, toddler group); four values (Knowledge, Spirituality, Community, Service). Closest in scale to a typical Gojoline customer.
- **adamscenter.org**: "Quick Donate" in header; six service cards with distinct verbs (Religious "Learn More", Funeral "Get Support", Education "Discover Programs"); trust block ("serving 30,000+ Muslims yearly across six branches"); sadaqah block explaining what gifts pay for, preset amounts. Prayer times only via third-party widget and PDF: slow and invisible, a weakness.
- **isbcc.org**: donate menu separates Zakat Al-Mal, Zakat Al-Fitr, Waqf, Sustainers, Udhiya; Qur'an verse in the announcement strip; parking notice near the top.
- Failed to load: icnyu.org (503), londoncentralmosque.org.uk (host not reachable from this session). Template galleries were not opened.

## Shared patterns

**Hero**: architecture or prayer-hall photo, mosque name, one welcoming line, and the prayer strip attached to the bottom edge of the hero (or immediately below). Buttons: "Prayer times" and "Donate"; optional "Plan your visit".

**Home page order (recommended)**
1. Header: name (Amharic/English, Arabic optional), Hijri + Gregorian (and Ethiopian) date, "Donate".
2. Hero with next-prayer highlight ("Asr in 42 min").
3. Today's prayer times: five prayers, two values each (Adhan/begins and Iqamah/jama'at), plus Jumu'ah (khutbah time, language, khateeb).
4. Welcome / about the masjid, with imam name and photo of the building.
5. Services cards: Nikah, Janazah, Madrasa/Qur'an classes, Shahada/new Muslims, counselling/ask the imam, hall hire.
6. Classes and weekly circles: day, time, audience (children, sisters, youth).
7. Donate: sadaqah, zakat, building/waqf, with what it pays for and local bank/Telebirr details.
8. News / announcements (Ramadan timetable, Eid prayer).
9. Gallery; contact with map, phone, opening hours.

**Signature elements**: five-column prayer strip with the next prayer highlighted; Jumu'ah pill; Hijri date; verse or hadith as an epigraph above the donate block; zakat/sadaqah split; janazah contact shown as a phone number available at any hour; subtle geometric pattern as a divider or card corner, never as a full background.

**Typography**: calm humanist sans for everything functional (times in tabular numerals, 18 to 22px, bold for iqamah); an elegant serif or high-contrast display only for the hero and section titles. Uppercase letter-spaced labels for prayer names (as Cambridge does) work in Latin; set Amharic labels in normal case.

**Palettes**
- A "Emerald and brass": primary `#0F6B4F`, dark accent `#0A3B2E`, light background `#F7F4EA`, text `#17231E`; optional brass `#C9A24B`.
- B "Harar dusk": primary `#1F5F78` teal blue, dark accent `#14263B` night indigo, light background `#F4F1E8` limewash, text `#1A2230`; optional clay `#D98E4A`.

**Photo style**: architecture and light, not faces: prayer hall with carpet lines, minaret at dusk, courtyard arches, mihrab detail, Qur'an on a stand, wudu, children reading. People from behind or at a distance. Deep shadows and warm highlights give text a natural dark area.

**What converts on mobile**: prayer times readable without scrolling or loading a widget (render as text, cache for offline); 5 columns collapse to a 2-column list with the next prayer first; sticky "Prayer times / Donate" bar; tap-to-call for janazah and nikah; donation shows copyable account numbers.

## Do
1. Render today's prayer times as real text at the top, with Jumu'ah separate.
2. Give each service its own card and action (book, arrange, enrol, call).
3. Split giving into sadaqah, zakat and building fund, and say what each funds.
4. Show Hijri date and highlight the next prayer.
5. Use one geometric motif and one accent colour, consistently.

## Don't
1. Don't depend on a third-party prayer widget or a PDF timetable as the only source.
2. Don't use close-up faces of worshippers in prayer or women's areas without consent; prefer architecture.
3. Don't run rotating banner carousels of event posters as the hero (ELM and Green Lane do; headlines get lost).
