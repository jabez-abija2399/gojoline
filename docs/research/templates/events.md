# Events niche brief (wedding / event planner, decor, venue, event photographer)

Method note: pages were fetched as text, so structure, copy, CTAs and forms below are observed; typeface and colour statements are niche conventions and my own recommendation, not measured from the CSS.

## Sites opened and what I took from each
- **mindyweiss.com**: press strip (Harper's Bazaar, Vogue) directly under the header, then a short bio with one button ("Meet Mindy"), gallery tiles with no captions, quotes at the bottom. Lesson: proof first, founder second, photos carry the rest.
- **bluebirdproductions.com**: hero is three words ("weddings + events / planning, design, production") + one CTA; three service tiles (weddings, social, corporate); testimonial carousel with "1 / 4" counter. Lesson: split by event type in 3 tiles.
- **calderclark.com**: almost no UI: H1 "Destination Weddings & Private Events", three one-line statements, one tagline. Lesson: very little copy, very confident.
- **colincowie.com**: secondary tab bar "WEDDINGS | CORPORATE | MILESTONE | DESTINATIONS", per-office phone + email, "Event Inquiry" in the nav. Weak: no button in the hero, broken Instagram feed. Lesson: never depend on a third-party feed.
- **emmajoytheweddingplanner.co.uk**: full-bleed table-setting photo, uppercase tracked headline "LUXURY WEDDING PLANNER", a couple's quote placed right under the hero, 3 service tiles, portfolio grid with "VIEW PORTFOLIO", "Meet Emma" with headshot, second quote, "As seen in", enquire button.
- **2706events.com (Lagos)**: 6-photo hero slider, nav "Who We Are / How We Work / Services / Testimonials / Portfolio / Say Hello", testimonial immediately after hero, photo collage. Lesson: "How we work" and "Say hello" are the two nav items that matter.
- **Colorlib roundup of 33 planner sites** (secondary source): recurring traits are full-bleed photo hero, gallery-led pages, three tiers (full planning / partial / day-of), press + testimonials, lots of white space.
- Failed to load: Squarespace and Webflow template galleries (no usable content), Emma Joy contact page (404).

## Hero pattern
Full-bleed photograph (a styled venue or table, not a posed couple), dark gradient at the bottom, 2 to 5 word headline, one line naming city and event types, ONE primary button ("Check your date" / "Enquire"). The best sites had no slider and no video; a single still is also right for slow connections. Luxury sites often omit the hero button: for small businesses, keep it.

## Home page order (top to bottom)
1. Header: wordmark, 4 links, Enquire button.
2. Hero.
3. Proof strip: "120+ weddings · since 2016 · Addis, Bishoftu, Hawassa" (replaces press logos, which a small business lacks).
4. Event types: 3 or 4 photo tiles (Wedding, Melse / traditional, Corporate, Birthday and graduation).
5. Editorial photo grid (6 to 8 images, mixed portrait/landscape, no captions) + "See all work".
6. Packages: 3 cards with "from ETB ..." and 4 to 5 inclusions each.
7. How it works: 4 numbered steps (Tell us the date, Meet and plan, We design and book, The day).
8. One large couple quote (names + month/year + venue).
9. About: planner portrait + 3 sentences.
10. Enquiry form + contacts. Footer.

## Signature UI
Asymmetric editorial photo grid; oversized italic serif pull-quote with couple's names in small caps; package cards with a thin rule and "from" price; numbered steps in large serif numerals; small-caps tracked eyebrow labels; thin hairlines instead of boxes and shadows.

## Typography
High-contrast serif display (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display class) at 32 to 40 px on mobile, sentence case, tight leading; italics for one emphasised word. Labels: sans, 11 to 12 px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.12 to 0.2em. Body: humanist sans 16 px. Amharic: no italics, no uppercase, no letter-spacing on Ethiopic; pair with Noto Serif Ethiopic for headings and Noto Sans Ethiopic for body, and allow 15% more line height.

## Palettes
- **Tej & Ivory** (warm, classic): primary gold `#B8893B`, dark accent `#3A2A1E`, background `#FBF6EE`, text `#2B2420`.
- **Enset Green** (garden, modern): primary deep green `#2F4A3C`, dark accent `#1E2B25`, background `#F6F3EA`, text `#22271F`; small blush highlight `#D9A79A`.
Gold on ivory fails contrast for body text: use gold only for rules, numerals and large headings; buttons are the dark accent with ivory text.

## Photo style
Warm, slightly soft, natural or candle light. Subjects: venue wide shots, table and floral details, joyful candid groups (dancing, procession, petals), one bride portrait, habesha dress and cultural moments. Crops: 3:2 landscape for hero, 4:5 portrait for grid tiles. Avoid flash-lit snapshots and heavy filters.

## What converts on mobile
Sticky bottom bar with two buttons: Call and Telegram (WhatsApp as a third option). Enquiry form with only: name, phone, event type (select), date, guest count (select of ranges: under 100, 100 to 300, 300 to 600, 600+), venue booked? (yes/no), message (optional). Promise a reply time ("we answer within a day"). Every package card ends with "Ask about this package", which pre-fills the form.

## Do
1. One still hero photo, compressed, with a text-safe dark area.
2. Show "from" prices in Birr on packages; it filters enquiries.
3. Put a real couple's quote within the first two screens.
4. Split services by event type, with a photo each.
5. Ask for date and guest count in the form; keep phone as the only required contact.

## Don't
1. No auto-playing sliders, video backgrounds or embedded Instagram feeds.
2. No script/calligraphy fonts for headings (unreadable, and no Ethiopic equivalent).
3. No walls of text about "our philosophy": three sentences maximum per section.
