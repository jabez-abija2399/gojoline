## Hero pattern
Large warm photo of pupils and a teacher in class (faces visible, uniforms, daylight). Small kicker ("Welcome to" / "Admissions open 2019 E.C."), school name or a one-line promise, one sentence with levels + location ("KG to Grade 8 in Bole, Addis Ababa"). Buttons: primary **Apply / Enquire now**, secondary **Book a visit**. Optional admissions-open badge.

## Home page order
1. Notice bar: "Registration for [year] is open" + phone
2. Header: logo/crest, 5 links, Enquire button
3. Hero
4. Stats band (4 numbers: students, teacher ratio, years, exam pass rate)
5. Programmes by age/grade (3-4 photo cards with age range)
6. Why us (4 short pillars with icons) + Director's welcome with portrait
7. Admissions in 3-4 numbered steps + fees/requirements link
8. School life gallery (sports, arts, labs)
9. Term dates / upcoming events list (3-4 rows with date blocks)
10. Parent testimonials
11. Enquiry form (parent name, phone, child's grade) + map
12. Footer: address, phones, Telegram, office hours, licence/accreditation

For training centres swap 5 for course cards (duration, schedule, fee, next intake) and 10 for graduate outcomes.

## Signature elements
Age/grade programme cards; numbered admissions steps; stats band; date-block calendar rows; crest in header; Director's welcome with signature; parent quotes; accreditation/licence line; fees table link.

## Typography and colour
(Inferred from knowledge of these sites; text fetches do not expose fonts.) Schools: serif headings with a clean sans body gives heritage and seriousness; kindergartens: rounded friendly sans, bigger radii. Small uppercase kicker above large title is the recurring device. Headline 30-34 px mobile / 48-60 px desktop; body 17 px.
- **Palette A "Crest navy and gold"** (primary/secondary school): primary #1E3A8A, dark accent #0B1F4B, light background #FBF8F1, text #1F2937 (gold highlight #F2B705)
- **Palette B "Sunny green"** (kindergarten / primary): primary #15803D, dark accent #14532D, light background #FFFBEB, text #1C1917 (sun accent #F59E0B)

## Photo style
Happy, dignified, engaged children: hands up, reading together, lab or robotics work, playground, art. Clean uniforms, eye-level camera, natural light, teacher present in at least one. Avoid charity-style close-ups and worn-out rooms.

## Mobile conversion
Sticky bottom bar: **Call** + **Enquire** (opens short form or Telegram). Admissions-open bar at top. Three-field enquiry form. Tap-to-call office number, office hours beside it. Fees/requirements as a light page or PDF link, not a wall of text.

## Do
1. Say levels, ages and location in the hero sentence.
2. Show programmes as age/grade cards with photos.
3. Make admissions three or four numbered steps ending in a button.
4. Put four honest numbers in a band (ratio, results, years, students).
5. Publish term dates and the registration period on the home page.

## Don't
1. No long history/mission text above the fold.
2. No poverty or charity imagery; no children's full names.
3. No portal logins or ten-item menus on the public home page.
