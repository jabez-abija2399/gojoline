# Clinic template brief (private clinic / dental / pharmacy / health centre)

## Sites opened (and what was taken)
- **Tend dental** (hellotend.com): hero = benefit headline ("Look forward to going to the dentist") + "Book Now" + click-to-call phone button side by side. Location cards show open/closed status, rating, address, phone, Book. Insurance list. "Over 8,000 five star reviews" stat. Team block as 2x2 icon grid.
- **Carbon Health**: hero = full-width photo of clinician with patient, "Book an Appointment", plus search by symptom/service. Numbered 01-04 feature cards (same-day booking, chat with team). "We accept most insurance" band with logos. Testimonials with name + clinic.
- **Cleveland Clinic**: quick-action row straight under hero (Providers / Locations / Appointments). Services as icon cards with one-line taglines. Two phone cards ("Get Care", "Need Help?") with numbers printed large. Phone in top bar and footer.
- **Parsley Health**: emotional headline in the patient's words, one CTA; "conditions we treat" grid of 11 cards each listing examples; FAQ accordion (insurance question first).
- **Aga Khan University Hospital, Nairobi**: "World Class Care, Here and Now" + "Find a Doctor"; Book an Appointment in header; accreditation logo row; stats tiles (700,000+ patients, 54 centres); patient stories. Weakness: no emergency number or hours on the page.
- **Nordic Medical Centre, Addis Ababa**: 12-card services grid with photo + "Learn More" (Emergency Room, Laboratory, Pharmacy, Imaging, Check-ups). "24/7" claim, short code 8901. Weaknesses to beat: welcome-only hero with no button, phone only in footer, mission/vision text before services, no doctors, no hours, no Telegram/WhatsApp.
- Partial/failed: Nairobi Hospital (only contact block rendered: many phone lines, no labelled emergency number); One Medical and Mayo Clinic returned 403; Webflow/Squarespace galleries gave no usable content.

## Hero pattern
Split layout on desktop, stacked on mobile: text first, photo of a real clinician (eye contact or with patient) second. Headline is a promise to the patient, 5-9 words ("Care you can trust, close to home"). One line of sub-text with speciality + area (e.g. "General practice, laboratory and pharmacy in Bole"). Two buttons: primary **Book an appointment**, secondary **Call 09XX XXX XXX** (tel: link). Under the buttons a one-line status: "Open today 8:00-20:00 · Emergency 24/7".

## Home page order
1. Top info strip: phone · hours today · emergency number (red dot)
2. Header: logo, 4-5 links, Book button
3. Hero (above)
4. Quick actions row (3 tiles): Book · Our doctors · Find us
5. Services grid (6-8 icon cards, one-line each, price "from ... Birr" optional)
6. Why us: 3-4 trust numbers (years, patients, doctors, lab results in X hours)
7. Doctors: photo cards with name, speciality, days available
8. Opening hours table + emergency box + map/address
9. Insurance / partner logos row (or "We accept" text list)
10. Patient testimonials (2-3, first name + area)
11. FAQ (payment, insurance, walk-ins, lab result times)
12. Final CTA band + footer with phone, Telegram, address, licence number

## Signature elements
Hours table with today highlighted; emergency number box; services icon grid; doctor cards (4:5 portrait, name, speciality, languages); trust-number band; "we accept" insurer row; licence/accreditation line.

## Typography and colour
(Inferred from knowledge of these brands; the text fetches do not expose fonts.) Humanist sans throughout, sentence case, large calm headline (32-36 px mobile / 48-56 px desktop), 17-18 px body, generous line height. Phone numbers set large and bold. No all-caps except tiny labels. Rounded 12-16 px cards, lots of white.
- **Palette A "Highland teal"**: primary #0F766E, dark accent #134E4A, light background #F0FDFA, text #0F172A (emergency accent #D92D20)
- **Palette B "Trust blue"**: primary #1D4ED8, dark accent #0B2A5B, light background #F5F8FC, text #1B2430 (warm accent #F59E0B for the secondary button)

## Photo style
Real-looking Black clinicians, smiling or attentive, clean light rooms, white/teal/blue clothing. Clinician-with-patient for hero; cropped portraits for doctors; lab microscope, pharmacy shelf, dental chair for service cards. Bright, slightly cool, no blood, no needles, no surgery.

## Mobile conversion
Sticky bottom bar with two buttons: **Call** (tel:) and **Book** (or Telegram). Floating Telegram/WhatsApp button if no booking form. Tap-to-call on every phone number. Hours and "open now" visible without scrolling. Map button opens Google Maps directions. Booking form: name, phone, service, preferred day; nothing more.

## Do
1. Put phone, hours and emergency number in the first screen.
2. Offer two actions everywhere: book and call.
3. Show named doctors with speciality and working days.
4. Describe services in patient words ("Children's health", not "Paediatrics dept") with Amharic equivalents.
5. Prove trust with numbers, licence, insurers and real testimonials.

## Don't
1. No mission/vision essay before services.
2. No frightening imagery (surgery, syringes, blood) or cold stock of unrelated faces.
3. No carousel hero or auto-video on slow connections; one compressed image.
