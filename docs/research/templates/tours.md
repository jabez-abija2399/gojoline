# Tours (tour operator / travel company): design brief

Method note: pages were read as text (structure, copy, CTAs, card fields). Fonts and colours could not be measured that way; the typography and palette sections are my recommendations informed by prior knowledge of these brands, not values sampled this session.

## Sites opened and what I took from each
- **intrepidtravel.com**: full-width photo hero with short headline + one button; search (destination + dates) directly under it; three proof taglines (number of trips, countries, founding year). **Trip card**: photo, rating + review count, badge (New / Premium / Sale), title, days, was/now price, next departure. Then "What sets us apart" (6 features), popular destinations grid, stories.
- **gadventures.com** (sale landing at the time): "Explore by region" image tiles, a 3-step "Not sure where to go?" helper, travel-style tiles, 6 icon-led "Why travel with us" blocks, Trustpilot row.
- **muchbetteradventures.com**: hero "Do Wild Things." + one sentence + "View All Adventures"; tabs for Destinations / Activities. Trip card adds **difficulty level (1 to 7)**, nights, from-price, rating (4.9 · 158 reviews), status badge. Dedicated "Book with confidence" trust block (free date swap, financial protection, Trustpilot), stats ("75% join solo"), repeated "Live chat / Send enquiry" help widget.
- **tesfatours.com** (Ethiopia): eyebrow + "Experience the real Ethiopia" + "Experts in Ethiopia travel"; four package cards with **"6 days / 5 nights" and "from $1,698"**; brand story built on the Amharic word (ተስፋ, hope); four value cards; TripAdvisor button; "Since 2010" and community-owned guesthouses as trust.
- **brilliant-ethiopia.com**: header with click-to-call phone + "Enquire Now"; hero with two buttons ("Find Your Adventure" / "Make an enquiry") and Trustpilot; "3 types of adventure" (set itinerary / tailor-made / luxury); popular tours with a **currency switcher**; destinations (Lalibela, Simien, Danakil, Omo, Tigray); closing 3-step process "Listen, Match, Reserve".
- **wildfrontierstravel.com Ethiopia page**: gallery hero; "Best experiences" and "Top places" cards; **named expert with photo and direct phone**; accordion of practical info (health, money, etiquette, when to go).
- Not readable: blacktomato.com (403, two attempts), ethiopianquadrants.com (bot check), one Much Better Adventures trip page (404). The day-by-day itinerary guidance below is therefore from general knowledge of these sites, not from a page read this session.

## Hero pattern
One full-bleed landscape with a person or track for scale, dark gradient. Eyebrow ("Ethiopia tours since 2012"), 3 to 6 word headline in an active voice, one sentence. Primary button "See our tours", secondary "Plan my trip" (opens enquiry/WhatsApp). Big operators add a search bar; a small operator with 4 to 10 tours should not: show the tours instead.

## Home page order
1. Header: logo, EN/አማ, phone/WhatsApp icon, Enquire button
2. Hero
3. Proof strip: rating, years operating, travellers hosted, licence
4. Featured tours: 3 to 6 trip cards
5. Destinations tiles (Lalibela, Simien, Danakil, Omo, Lake Tana, Addis)
6. Why travel with us: 4 icon blocks (local guides, small groups, tailor-made, fair prices)
7. How it works: 3 steps (tell us, we plan, you travel)
8. Sample itinerary: day-by-day accordion for the flagship tour
9. Guide / founder story with a real portrait
10. Reviews (TripAdvisor / Google, named, with country)
11. Enquiry form (dates, group size, interests) + WhatsApp, Telegram, phone
12. Footer: licence number, address, payment methods, socials

## Signature UI elements
- **Trip card**: 3:2 photo with a badge (Best seller / New), title, chips for **duration ("8 days")**, **difficulty** (Easy / Moderate / Hard) and group size, route line ("Addis → Lalibela → Gondar"), "from ETB / USD x per person", rating.
- **Itinerary timeline**: numbered days on a vertical line, each "Day 3 · Simien trek to Geech", collapsible, with meals/overnight icons.
- Included / not included two-column tick and cross list.
- Destination tiles: photo with name overlaid, number of tours.
- Currency toggle (ETB / USD) because customers are both local and foreign.

## Typography
Confident geometric or grotesque sans for headings (Poppins, Sora, Manrope ExtraBold), sentence case, 30 to 38 px on mobile, tight tracking (-0.01 to -0.02em); body sans 16 px. Chips and badges 12 px uppercase, semi-bold, tracking 0.06em. An optional hand-drawn or serif italic accent word in the hero adds warmth. Ethiopic fallback: Noto Sans Ethiopic, weights 400/700, line-height about 1.5.

## Palettes
- **Simien Trail** (green + sun): primary `#1F6B4F` highland green, dark accent `#12332A` deep forest, light background `#F5F1E8` parchment, text `#1D2420`; use `#E9A23B` amber only for badges and prices.
- **Danakil Sunset** (ochre + night): primary `#D2691E` burnt orange, dark accent `#1B2A41` indigo night, light background `#FBF6EE` sand, text `#22201D`.

## Photo style
Big landscapes with depth and weather, people small in frame for scale; local people photographed with dignity, doing something (walking, guiding, trading), not posing for tourists; one icon image per destination (rock-hewn church, gelada, sulphur springs, falls). Saturated but natural colour. Avoid crowds of foreign tourists and vehicles with logos.

## What converts on mobile
Sticky bottom bar: "Enquire" + WhatsApp. Price-from and duration visible on the card without tapping. Short enquiry form (when, how many, which tour, phone). Rating badge near every CTA. Click-to-call in the header. Named human ("Talk to Abebe, your guide") instead of an anonymous form.

## Do
1. Put duration, difficulty and from-price on every trip card.
2. Show a day-by-day itinerary and an included/excluded list for each tour.
3. Give reviews with name, country and source; show licence/association membership.
4. Offer tailor-made as an explicit option with a 3-step process.
5. Use one iconic photo per destination and name the place on the image.

## Don't
1. No search/filter UI for a catalogue of under ten tours.
2. No hero carousel of sale banners or autoplay video on slow connections.
3. No vague "contact us for price"; no photos that exoticise people or show them without context.
