# Gym / fitness studio / yoga & aerobics / martial arts / sports club — design brief

Researched 2026-11-18. One template with three moods (gym, studio, dojo), the same skeleton: price, timetable, trainers, free trial.

## Sites opened and what I took

Three plan cards side by side: Off-Peak £18.99 (limited hours), Core £24.99 ("Access the gym anytime"), Plus £31.99 tagged "Best value" (multi-gym, guest visits, freeze). One shared line under all cards: "No contract - Cancel any time". Joining fee shown, "Pricing may differ by location".

- **thirdspace.london** (premium club) — "Training for life" + two buttons "Find your club" / "Become a member". Facilities named concretely (pools, HIIT, cycle, yoga, Reformer, combat zones). Class cards with names (Afterburner, Metaburn…), "View the timetable". Enquiry is a "Book a tour" modal, not a price.

## Hero pattern
Photo-led with text on a dark gradient (bottom 60%, so text stays readable): a real member mid-movement in the actual gym. Eyebrow with type + area ("Gym & aerobics · Sarbet"). Headline 4–7 words about the feeling or result, not the equipment ("Get stronger with people who know your name"). One line with the facts: hours + women's sessions + price anchor ("Open 5:30–22:00 · Women-only 9–12 daily · From 1,200 Birr/month"). Primary button **Try a free session** (opens the trial form), secondary **See prices**. No autoplay video on mobile; one compressed still (≤120 KB).

## Home page order
1. Header: logo, Prices, Timetable, Trainers, Gallery; Free trial button
2. Hero (above)
3. Fact strip (4 chips): open hours · women-only hours · parking/shower/locker · since year
4. Prices: 3–4 plan cards — 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months; per-month equivalent and "save X Birr" on long plans; registration fee in one line under the cards; day pass if offered; student/couple/family note
5. Today's classes (timetable preview) + "Full week" link
6. Programmes: 4–6 tiles (Weights, Aerobics, Spinning, Yoga, Boxing/Taekwondo, Kids) with one line and level
7. Trainers: portrait cards, name, speciality, certificate, languages, "Ask Abel" Telegram link
8. Results: 2–3 member stories (first name, months, what changed, quote); optional before/after pair with consent label
9. Facilities gallery: 6 photos in a swipe row (floor, studio, showers/lockers, sauna, parking, entrance)
10. Free trial form band
11. FAQ: what to bring, women's hours, can I freeze, payment (cash, mobile money, bank transfer), age limit
12. Find us: map link, landmark, hours table (men/mixed and women rows)
13. Footer: phone, Telegram, address, trade licence, "Report a problem" link

## Signature UI
- **Plan cards**: duration as the big label, price in Birr in large tabular figures, "≈ 1,000 Birr/month" under long plans, one "Most popular" tag, the same 3–4 included items on each card with ticks; choose-plan button opens Telegram with the plan pre-filled
- **Timetable**: day tabs (today selected by default, Mon–Sun in Amharic and English), each row = time · class · trainer · level dot · badge ("Women only", "Kids"); filter chips for class type; it is a list, never a 7-column grid on mobile
- **Women-only hours** shown as its own row in the hours table and as a badge on classes
- **Trainer cards** 4:5 portraits with speciality chips
- **Free trial form**: name, phone, goal (lose weight / get stronger / fitness / self-defence), preferred time (morning / evening / women's session); sends to Telegram; confirmation "We'll call you today to book your session"
- **Results card**: photo or initials, first name, "4 months", one sentence; consent tag
- **Facilities swipe row** with captions

## Before/after and results
Optional section, off by default. Each before/after pair needs the member's written consent (the owner ticks "I have permission"), same pose and light, first name only, timeframe stated ("5 months, 3 sessions a week"). No weight-loss promises in headlines, no shirtless shock imagery, no shaming words. Prefer story cards and simple numbers that are true for this gym ("120 members", "8 trainers", "Women's aerobics every morning since 2018").

## Typography
Strong condensed or grotesque display for headings (Barlow Condensed 700, Oswald, or Archivo 800) in English, with Noto Sans Ethiopic Bold for Amharic; Inter for body. English headings may be uppercase with +0.02em tracking; Amharic never. Hero 36–40px at 360px, 64–80px desktop. Prices 32–40px, tabular. Timetable rows 16px with time in bold tabular figures. Yoga/studio mood swaps the display font for a soft serif (Fraunces) in sentence case.

## Palettes
- **Charcoal + volt (gym, CrossFit, boxing)**: primary `#C6F432` (buttons with dark text), dark accent `#111214`, light background `#F4F4F1`, text `#121212`
- **Ember + ink (aerobics, dance fitness, martial arts)**: primary `#E2452B`, dark accent `#1A1414`, light background `#FFF6F2`, text `#1B1716` (women's-session badge `#B83280`)
- Yoga/studio variant: sage `#5E7C63`, clay `#C0704A`, background `#F7F4EE`, text `#22201C`

## Photo style
Real members of mixed ages and body types training in this gym, mid-movement, sweat and smiles; the trainer correcting form; a full aerobics class from the front; the women's session (with permission); clean showers and lockers; the entrance and sign. Warm, contrasty light, 4:5 portraits for people, 3:2 for rooms. No foreign stock bodybuilders, no empty dark rooms, no mirror selfies.

## What converts on mobile
- Sticky bottom bar: **Free trial** + **Telegram** (or Call)
- Prices visible within two scrolls, in Birr, with the registration fee stated
- Today's classes shown on the home page, not behind a link
- Women-only hours in the first screen when the gym offers them
- Trial form of 3–4 fields, one tap per choice (chips, not dropdowns)
- Gallery as swipe, images lazy-loaded at ≤80 KB each

## Do
1. Put prices in Birr on the home page, with plan lengths people actually buy (1, 3, 6, 12 months).
2. Show today's classes and the women-only hours without a click.
3. Name every trainer with a photo, speciality and certificate.
4. Make the free trial a 3-field form that lands in the owner's Telegram.
5. Use true, local proof: member count, years open, named member stories with consent.

## Don't
1. No "call for price", no hidden registration fee, no prices that differ between sections.
2. No autoplay video, no full-screen slider, no 7-column timetable on a phone.
3. No before/after photos without recorded consent, and no weight-loss guarantees.
