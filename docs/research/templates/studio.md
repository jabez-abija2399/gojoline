# Studio / wedding vendor site (photographer, videographer, decor, wedding planner, event equipment rental) — design brief

Scope note: `events.md` covers the planner-led "event business" site (event-type tiles, one still hero, editorial grid). This template is portfolio-first: the work is the page, each wedding is its own story, and the main action is "Is my date free?". Rental businesses use the same shell with a catalogue in place of stories. Pages were fetched as text; layout, copy, CTAs and fields are observed, typefaces and colours are my recommendation.

## Hero pattern
The photo is the hero, full-bleed edge to edge, one strong frame (couple walking, procession, a decor wide shot), 4:5 on mobile so it fills the phone, dark gradient only along the bottom third. Over it: business name or role + city ("Wedding photography · Addis Ababa & worldwide"), one 4–7 word line, ONE button "Check your date". Directly under the photo, a thin strip: "180+ weddings · since 2015 · Addis, Bahir Dar, DC". For rental, swap the photo for a lit tent/hall setup and the button for "See the catalogue".

## Home page order
1. Header: wordmark, Work, Packages, About, Contact; "Check your date" button; language switch አማ / EN
2. Hero (one photo, no slider)
3. Proof strip: weddings covered, years, cities/countries
4. Featured stories: 3–6 wedding cards, each = cover photo, couple names, place, month + year, chips for what was covered (Kelekel · Wedding · Melse)
5. Packages: 3 tiers with "from ETB" and inclusions (see below)
6. Availability enquiry: date picker first, then the short form
7. Couple quotes: 2–3, both names + city ("Hana & Dawit · Washington DC")
8. How booking works: 4 steps (Send your date → Call/video call → Deposit holds the date → Day plan 2 weeks before)
9. About: the photographer/decorator portrait at work + 3 sentences + team (second shooter, videographer, drone)
10. For couples abroad: one band (see below)
11. FAQ: 5–6 questions
12. Footer: phone, Telegram, address/studio hours, socials, licence/TIN, report-abuse link

Story page: names + place + date → one wide photo → day-by-day sections (Kelekel, Wedding, Melse) each with 6–12 images → 2-line caption per section → vendors credited → couple quote → "Check your date" → previous/next story.
Rental variant: 3 replaces 4 with category tiles (Tents, Chairs & tables, Décor & linen, Lighting, Sound & stage, Generators); every item = photo, name, size/capacity, price per day/event, "Add to quote list".

## Signature UI
- Full-bleed story covers, one per row on mobile, names in a refined serif over a bottom gradient
- Event chips on stories: Kelekel · Telosh · Wedding · Melse · Engagement shoot
- Story gallery: masonry of 2 columns on mobile, tap to open a swipe viewer with counter "12 / 48"; no captions on images, caption once per section
- Package cards with "from ETB 35,000", hours, people, deliverables (edited photos count, album pages, film length), turnaround ("photos in 4 weeks"), "Ask about this package"
- Date-first availability widget: month picker with days marked "Booked" (owner-set), big "Is [date] free?" button
- Quote list (rental): sticky count "4 items · Send for a quote", sent to Telegram, no cart or checkout
- Vendor credits list under each story

## Typography
Photos carry the design, type stays quiet. Headings in a refined, light-to-regular serif (Newsreader, Libre Caslon Display, DM Serif Display used sparingly) for couple names and section titles; everything else in a clean geometric sans (Jost, DM Sans, Outfit) at 16px. Labels and chips: sans 12px, uppercase, +0.1em tracking in Latin. Couple names on covers 28–32px at 360px, 48–64px desktop. Prices tabular, 20–24px. Different from `events.md`: no high-contrast display serif, no oversized italic pull-quotes, more white (or black) space and smaller type. Amharic: Noto Serif Ethiopic for names/headings, Noto Sans Ethiopic body, no uppercase or tracking, +15% line height; couple names shown in both scripts when given.

## Palettes
- **Darkroom (photo/video studios)**: primary `#C46A3A` (warm clay for buttons and chips), dark accent `#121212`, light background `#F4F1EC`, text `#1A1A1A`. Story pages and the gallery viewer flip to the dark accent background so photos glow.
- **Linen & kemis (decor, planners, rental)**: primary `#2F5D62` (deep teal), dark accent `#1C2B2D`, light background `#FAF8F4`, text `#202626` (thread highlight `#B08D57`, taken from tibeb borders, for rules and small chips only).
White text on `#C46A3A` passes only at 18px bold+; use the dark accent for small-text buttons.

## Photo style
Real weddings only, never stock. Candid moments over posed: the groom's party arriving to fetch the bride, kelekel ring moment, gabi and kaba in the melse, coffee ceremony, elders blessing, dancing (eskista), bride's tibeb close-up, decor wide shots before guests arrive. Warm, true skin tones (avoid orange grading on darker skin), no heavy vignettes. Covers 4:5, gallery mixed 2:3 and 3:2. Compress hard (WebP ~120 KB per gallery image, lazy load, blurred placeholder); first story cover must load in under 2 s on 3G.

## Packages, availability and diaspora
- Tiers (photo example): **Kelekel only** (4 hours, 1 photographer, 150 edited photos) · **Wedding day** (10 hours, 2 photographers + video highlight 3–5 min, 500 photos) · **Full celebration** (Kelekel + Wedding + Melse, photo + video + 30-page album). Decor tiers by guest count; rental by item. Prices "from ETB", integer Birr, no cents.
- Availability form: date (required) · which events (Kelekel / Wedding / Melse / other, multi) · venue or city · guest count range · package · name · phone or Telegram · "Where do you live now?" (Ethiopia / abroad + country) · note. Sends to the owner's Telegram; reply promise "within 24 hours".
- Booking is held by a deposit (e.g. 30%) paid by bank transfer or Telebirr; the site explains this, it never takes a card. Owner marks booked days.
- Couples abroad band: "Planning from abroad?" — video-call planning, a family member can meet us and pay the deposit in Birr, we coordinate with your planner/decorator, all files delivered online (download link), album shipped or collected. Show the season: book 3–6 months ahead for January (after Timket) and the weeks after Fasika; no weddings during the Orthodox fasts.

## What converts on mobile
- Sticky bottom bar: "Check your date" + Telegram (call as third icon)
- One story visible within the first two screens
- Package cards stacked, each ending in a pre-filling "Ask about this package"
- Form starts with the date; phone or Telegram is the only required contact
- Gallery viewer with swipe and a visible "Check your date" in its toolbar

## Do
1. Make every gallery a named wedding story with place, month and which events were covered.
2. Show "from" prices in Birr and what each tier delivers (hours, people, photo count, turnaround).
3. Ask for the date first; mark booked dates so couples self-filter.
4. Name Ethiopian moments (kelekel, melse, telosh) and show them; they are what local couples book for.
5. Speak to couples abroad in one clear band: video call, family deposit in Birr, online delivery.

## Don't
1. No sliders, autoplay video, music, or embedded Instagram feeds (slow, and they break).
2. No right-click blocking, watermarks across the whole photo or giant uncompressed images.
3. No online card checkout or cart; rental "quote list" goes to Telegram.
4. No stock wedding photos or other vendors' work; every image must be the owner's.
