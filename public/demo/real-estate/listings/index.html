What is around

Know the neighbourhood before you visit

Close to the airport and the cafés

Apartments above shops and offices, ten minutes from the airport. Busy by day, lively at night.

City-centre living, walk to work

Tall apartment buildings among offices, hotels and ministries. Most of our furnished rentals are here.

More space and green for families

Newer compounds on the east side of the city, with gardens, schools and quieter streets. More space for your Birr than in Bole.

Quiet streets, houses with gardens

Villas and G+1 houses in walled compounds, close to international schools and embassies.
