Welcome

A family lodge, kept the slow way

We built Tana Shore for travellers who want the lake, not the noise. Rooms are spread through an old garden, and every one is a short walk from the water.

Breakfast is cooked to order, the coffee ceremony starts at four, and our own boat leaves for the island monasteries each morning.

  • Breakfast and Wi-Fi included in every room
  • Free pickup from the airport
  • Boat trips to the monasteries, arranged at reception
