Good to know

Delivery, payment and exchange

  • Delivery in Addis

    1 to 2 days, 200 Birr. Free above 10,000 Birr.

  • Other towns

    Sent by bus or post. You pay the carrier's fee.

  • Pay on delivery

    Cash or Telebirr. Nothing to pay before you see it.

  • Free adjustments

    We shorten or take in any dress you buy from us.

  • Exchange within 7 days

    Unworn, with the receipt. Made-to-measure pieces are adjusted instead.

  • Gift wrapping

    Free, in cotton cloth with a hand-written card.

Questions before you order

How do I know my size?

Send us your height and the size you usually wear. If it does not fit, we adjust it free.

Can I see it before I pay?

Yes. You open the parcel with the rider and pay only if you are happy.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes, for ten pieces or more. Call the shop for the wholesale price.

Can you send abroad?

We hand your parcel to the courier you choose. You pay them directly.

Found your piece?

Send us its name. We answer within the hour during shop hours.
