The makers

Every piece has a weaver behind it

Our cloth is woven on wooden looms in Shiro Meda and Gullele, by families who have done this work for generations. A holiday kemis takes one weaver about five days.

We pay our weavers on the day they deliver, and our tailors finish every dress in the Merkato shop, where you can come and watch.
