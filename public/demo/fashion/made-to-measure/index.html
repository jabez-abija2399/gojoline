Questions

Before you order

How do I measure myself?

We send you a short guide on Telegram with pictures. A friend and a tape measure are enough.

How do I pay from abroad?

Family in Ethiopia can pay by Telebirr or bank transfer. Half when you order, half before we ship.

What if it does not fit?

We adjust it for free within a month. Send it back or bring it to the shop.
