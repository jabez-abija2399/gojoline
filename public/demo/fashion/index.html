Made to measure

From your measurements to your door

Families abroad order this way every week. You never need to come to Addis.

  1. 1

    Choose a style and fabric

    From the collection, or send us a photo of a dress you love.

  2. 2

    Send your measurements

    Eight numbers and a photo. We check them with you on Telegram before cutting.

  3. 3

    We weave and sew

    Three to six weeks, depending on the tibeb. You get photos along the way.

  4. 4

    Collect or we ship

    Pick it up in Shiro Meda, or we send it worldwide by courier.

Our looms

One dress, three weeks of weaving

Our weavers work on wooden looms in Shiro Meda, the way their families have for generations. The tibeb, the woven border, is made thread by thread.

That is why a Tsion dress lasts for years and can be passed down.
