Before you come

Good to know

When can I arrive and leave?

Your suite is ready from 2 pm and yours until noon on the day you leave. Ask us for earlier or later.

How do I pay?

We confirm your stay first. You pay at the house by bank transfer, Telebirr or card.

Are children welcome?

Yes, children of every age. The Acacia Suite takes an extra bed.
