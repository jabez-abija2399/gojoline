The house

A private house, kept for a few guests at a time

Wanza Hill began as a family house on the ridge. We kept its high thatched roofs, its cane chairs and its view, and made room for no more than twenty-four guests.

There is no lobby and no queue. Someone who knows your name meets you at the door, and the rest of the day is yours.

Experiences

Days shaped around you

Nothing is scheduled unless you ask. These are the things guests come back for.

An afternoon in the spa

Massages with oils pressed from Ethiopian herbs, a steam room of eucalyptus, and a quiet garden to rest in after.

Dinner under the acacia

On clear evenings we lay one long table in the open, and the kitchen cooks over fire from what the farm brought that morning.

The coffee ceremony

Every afternoon at four, beans are roasted, ground and poured from the jebena on the terrace. Stay for all three rounds.

Evenings on the veranda

Lanterns on the stone veranda, a fire when the hills turn cold, and the quiet of a house with very few guests.

The destination

The oldest highlands in Africa, a short flight away

From the house, our concierge arranges private journeys north: rock churches, castles, waterfalls and the roof of Ethiopia.
