From the director

We teach children, not just lessons

When we opened with forty children, we promised parents two things: your child will be safe, and your child will be seen. Fourteen years on, we still greet every pupil at the gate each morning.

We follow the national curriculum and add what we think matters: reading for pleasure, spoken English from kindergarten, and time to play, paint and build.

Come and see a normal school day. That will tell you more than any page can.

W/ro Almaz G., School Director

Good to know

Parents often ask

How old must my child be for KG 1?

Four years old by the first day of school in September.

How do I pay the fees?

Fees are paid each term, at the office or by bank transfer or Telebirr. The school year has four terms.

Is there school transport?

Yes. Our buses cover most of Adama for 1,800 Birr a term. Ask the office about your area.

Do children eat at school?

Children bring lunch from home and eat together with their teacher. Kindergarten children get a morning snack from us.

Can my child join in the middle of the year?

Yes, when there is a place in the class. Bring the last report card from the previous school.
