Beyond lessons

What makes the week full

  • A library that is used

    Every class has a library hour each week, and children take a book home every Friday. Amharic and English story books for every reading level.

  • Science lab

    Real experiments from Grade 5.

  • Computer room

    Typing and basic coding, one computer per child.

  • Art and music

    Every week, for every class.

  • Sport

    Football, volleyball and athletics on our own field.

  • School bus

    Door to gate, with an assistant on every bus.
