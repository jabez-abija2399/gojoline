Admissions

How to join us

Registration for the new school year runs from 8 July until classes are full.

  1. 1

    Ask or apply

    Send the short form or call the office. It takes two minutes.

  2. 2

    Visit the school

    See the classrooms, meet the director and bring your child along.

  3. 3

    A friendly assessment

    A short reading and number activity, so we place your child in the right class.

  4. 4

    Register

    Bring the documents, pay the registration fee and collect the uniform list.

Fees

Fees for this school year

In Birr. The school year has four terms. A second child gets 10% off tuition.

Tuition per term

  • Kindergarten (KG 1 to 3)

    9,500 Birr

    Books and morning snack included

  • Grades 1 to 4

    12,500 Birr

    Books included

  • Grades 5 to 8

    14,500 Birr

    Books and lab materials included

Other fees

  • Registration

    2,500 Birr

    Once a year

  • School bus

    1,800 Birr

    Per term, optional

  • Uniform set

    3,200 Birr

    Two shirts, trousers or skirt, sweater

Documents

What to bring

  • Birth certificate

    A copy is enough. We look at the original and give it back.

  • Four passport photos

    Of your child, taken recently.

  • Last report card

    For Grade 1 and above, from the previous school.

  • Vaccination card

    For kindergarten children.

Book a visit

Choose a weekday morning. The director or her deputy shows you round, and your child is welcome to come.
