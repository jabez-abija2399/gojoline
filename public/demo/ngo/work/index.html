Our work

Practical help, planned with the village

We work in 9 kebeles within an hour of Hawassa: Tula, Dore Bafano, Wondo Genet and their neighbours.

Programmes

What we do, and where

Four programmes that work together. Choose one to see it.

Girls in school

A school kit each September, tutoring three afternoons a week and a girls' club in every partner school.

Women's savings groups

Twenty women save together every week, lend to each other and start small businesses with training from our team.

Home gardens and seed

Improved seed, enset and vegetable gardens, and a farmer trainer who visits every household each month.

Trees for the lake hills

School and youth clubs raise seedlings and plant them on the bare slopes above Lake Hawassa every rainy season.

Results

What changed last school year

We count what matters to families and report it every September. Last year 312 girls received a school kit and joined tutoring. All but 13 of them moved up to the next grade.

Savings groups lent 1.9 million Birr to their own members, and nearly all of it was repaid on time.

Our story so far

  1. Fourteen partner schools

    2025

    Hawassa and Wondo Genet

    Tutoring now runs in every partner school, led by 46 volunteer tutors.

  2. Home gardens begin

    2022

    Dore Bafano

    Savings group members asked for seed and training. Sixty households started.

  3. First tree nursery

    2019

    Tula

    A school club raised 4,000 seedlings in its first season.

  4. First savings group

    2017

    Tula

    Eighteen mothers began saving 20 Birr a week.

  5. Tesfa Hawassa is founded

    2016

    Hawassa

    Six teachers and a group of parents buy school kits for 40 girls.
