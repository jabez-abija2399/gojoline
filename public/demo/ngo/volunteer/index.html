Ways to help

Choose what suits you

  • Tutor

    Maths, English or science for grades 5 to 10. Two afternoons a week at a school near you.

  • Bookkeeping trainer

    Help a savings group keep clear records. One meeting a week.

  • Planting days

    Join school clubs on Saturdays in June and July.

  • Skills from afar

    Photography, design, translation or accounting, from wherever you are.
