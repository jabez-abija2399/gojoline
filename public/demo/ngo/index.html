Who we are

Started by teachers and parents

In 2016 six teachers and a group of mothers in Hawassa asked a simple question: why do so many bright girls leave school after grade six? The answers were practical. No uniform, no time to study, no money at home.

So we work on all three. Girls get the materials and tutoring they need, and their mothers save and earn together. Every plan is made with the school and the kebele, and every project is run by people who live there.

  • Run by 11 local staff and 120 volunteers
  • Plans agreed with schools and kebele leaders
  • A printed report to every donor each year

What we do

Four programmes, one goal

Each one removes a reason a girl might leave school.

  • Largest programme

    Education

    Girls in school

    • 14 schools
    • Grades 5 to 10

    A school kit each September, tutoring three afternoons a week and a girls' club in every partner school.

  • Income

    Women's savings groups

    • 38 groups
    • 760 members

    Twenty women save together every week, lend to each other and start small businesses with training from our team.

  • Food

    Home gardens and seed

    • 410 households
    • 9 kebeles

    Improved seed, enset and vegetable gardens, and a farmer trainer who visits every household each month.

  • Land

    Trees for the lake hills

    • 64,000 seedlings
    • 3 nurseries

    School and youth clubs raise seedlings and plant them on the bare slopes above Lake Hawassa every rainy season.

Where gifts go

What happens to your gift

Out of every 100 Birr, 86 goes to programmes, 9 to running the office and 5 to raising funds.

  1. 01

    A receipt the same day

    We call you, confirm your gift and send a stamped receipt by Telegram or in person.

  2. 02

    Spent where you chose

    Your gift goes to the programme you picked. Kits are bought in Hawassa and handed out at school.

  3. 03

    A report every September

    What was bought, for which school or group, and what changed, with photos.

Before you give

How do I send my gift?

By Telebirr, bank transfer or in person at our office in Piazza. Fill in the form and we call you with the details.

Will I know how it was used?

Yes. You get a receipt at once and a report in September showing what was bought and for which school or group.

Can I give something other than money?

Yes. We welcome exercise books, sanitary pads, seedlings and your time as a tutor or trainer.

Can I visit a project?

Gladly. We visit a school and a savings group on the last Friday of each month. Call us to join.
