    Book or walk in

Good to know

Before you come

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can walk in any time we are open. With an appointment you are seen at your time instead of waiting your turn.

How much is a visit?

A doctor's visit is 400 Birr. Tests and medicine are extra, and we tell you the price before we do anything.

How can I pay?

Cash, Telebirr or bank transfer at the front desk.

When do I get lab results?

Most are ready in about two hours. A few special tests take one or two days; we call you when they arrive.

What if it is an emergency?

Call us straight away during opening hours. We are not open at night: for a serious emergency after hours, go to the nearest hospital.

Book an appointment

Leave your name and number. We call you back within one working hour to agree a time. For anything urgent, please call instead.
