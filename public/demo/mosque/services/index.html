Family services

Nikah, janazah and shahada

  • Marriage

    Nikah

    • Imam
    • Wali and two witnesses
    • Certificate

    The imam meets the couple and the bride's wali beforehand, then conducts the nikah in the masjid after Asr or at your home. Book at least two weeks ahead.

    1,500 Birroffice fee, with the certificateAsk the office
  • Any hour

    Funeral

    Janazah

    • Washing and shrouding
    • Janazah prayer
    • Help with burial

    Our volunteers, brothers and sisters, wash and shroud the deceased, and the congregation prays the janazah after the next prayer. There is no charge; families who are able give what they wish.

    Call the office

  • New Muslims

    Shahada and first steps

    • Free
    • One-to-one teaching

    Take your shahada with the imam, privately or after a prayer. A brother or sister then teaches you wudu, the prayer and Al-Fatiha at your own pace.

    Ask the office

Also at Al-Noor

More ways we serve

  1. 01

    Ask the imam

    Questions of worship, family and inheritance, answered in private after Asr or by appointment.

  2. 02

    Reconciliation

    Elders of the masjid sit with couples and families to settle disputes with patience.

  3. 03

    Ramadan iftar

    A shared iftar in the courtyard every evening of Ramadan, cooked by volunteers.

Booking a nikah

How to arrange a nikah

  1. 1

    Send a request

    Use the form below or come to the office with your preferred date.

  2. 2

    Meet the imam

    The couple and the wali come once before the day. Bring your kebele IDs.

  3. 3

    The day of the nikah

    Come with two witnesses and the agreed mahr. You leave with a signed certificate.

Ask the office
