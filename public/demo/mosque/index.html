Services

With you at every stage of life

Beyond the daily prayers, the masjid serves the families of Kolfe from the first lesson to the last farewell.

  • Nikah

    Marriage contracts conducted by the imam, in the masjid or at your home.

  • Janazah

    Washing, shrouding and the funeral prayer. Call us at any hour.

  • Madrasa

    Qur'an reading, tajwid and hifz for children and adults.

  • New Muslims

    Taking the shahada, and patient teaching of the basics afterwards.

  • Ask the imam

    Private questions and family counsel, after Asr or by appointment.

  • Family support

    Food and basic help for neighbours in need, given with dignity.

Classes and circles

Learning through the week

Every class is open to newcomers. Come once and listen before you decide.

  • 8:30 amChildren's madrasaSaturday and Sunday, ages 5 to 14. Reading, short surahs and good manners.
  • After MaghribAdults' Qur'an and tajwidMonday to Thursday. For beginners and for those improving their recitation.
  • 4:00 pmSisters' circleTuesday and Thursday. Qur'an and everyday fiqh with a woman teacher.
  • After FajrHifz circleEvery day. Memorisation and review with the ustaz.
  • After AsrWeekly darsEvery Sunday. Tafsir for everyone, in Amharic. Tea afterwards.

Places in the children's madrasa are limited, so please enrol before coming.

Our masjid

Built by the neighbourhood, open to all

Al-Noor began as one room where a few families of Kolfe prayed together. Today it holds a full congregation at every prayer, a madrasa and a kitchen that feeds the fasting through Ramadan.

It is still run the same way: by volunteers from the neighbourhood, and by what the congregation gives.

  • Sisters' prayer hall with its own entrance and wudu area
  • Friday khutbah in Amharic, with Arabic
  • A ramp at the side door for wheelchairs and elders
