Before you come

Everyone is welcome at Al-Noor

Whether you pray here every day or are visiting a masjid for the first time, come in. If you are not sure where to go, ask anyone at the gate.

Visitors who are not Muslim are welcome too. The best time is between the prayers, and school and neighbourhood groups can arrange a short tour with the office.

  • Shoes go on the racks by each door
  • Wudu areas for brothers and for sisters, with warm water in the mornings
  • Sisters enter by the side gate; scarves are available at the door
  • Please keep phones silent in the prayer hall
Prayer times

Questions people ask

When should I arrive for Jumu'ah?

The hall fills by 12:30 pm. Come before the adhan at 12:20 and make wudu at home if you can.

Is there space for sisters on Fridays?

Yes. The sisters' hall is open for every prayer, including Jumu'ah and Tarawih.

Is there parking?

Only a few spaces inside the gate. On Fridays please walk or come by taxi, and do not block the neighbours' gates.

How do I pay zakat through the masjid?

Choose Zakat in the giving form on the home page, or bring it to the office. Tell us it is zakat so it is recorded and given separately.
