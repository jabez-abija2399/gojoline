Questions about cakes

Do you make fasting cakes?

Yes, without eggs or butter, for the fasting seasons. Ask when you order.

How far ahead should I order?

Two days for most cakes, two weeks for weddings, and earlier before holidays.

Do you deliver?

Yes, anywhere in Addis Ababa. The fee depends on the distance.
