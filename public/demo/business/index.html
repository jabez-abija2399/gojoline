What we do

Six services, one team

From the first idea to the day the loan is approved, and the books that follow.

  • Feasibility studies

    Market, technical and financial study of a new project, in the format banks and the investment commission ask for.

  • Business plans

    A clear plan with five-year figures, ready for a loan or an investor.

  • Market research

    Surveys, competitor checks and demand estimates from the field, not from a desk.

  • Accounting and tax

    Monthly books, VAT and payroll filed on time, so there are no penalties.

  • Licences and permits

    Company registration, trade licence and investment permit, handled from start to finish.

  • People and structure

    Job descriptions, salary scales and a staff manual that fit the labour law.
