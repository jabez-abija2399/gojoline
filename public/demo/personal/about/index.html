About Hana

Designer, printer's apprentice, Addis born

I grew up near Piassa, where every shop sign was painted by hand. I still think a good brand should be simple enough to paint on a wall.

I learned the craft at a print studio, led identity work at an agency in Bole, and went independent in 2022. Since then I have worked with cafés, architects, makers and a magazine.

Ge'ez script is not an afterthought in my work. I draw and set Amharic first, so it never looks like a translation.
