Before you come

Do I need to book a table?

No, just walk in. Book only for the full ceremony or for six people or more.

Cash, Telebirr or bank transfer.

Is there parking?

There are a few places in front, and guarded street parking beside Edna Mall.

Do you deliver?

Not yet. You can order by phone and collect, or send a rider to pick it up.
