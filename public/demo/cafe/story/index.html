The ceremony

Three rounds, no hurry

Abol, tona, bereka: the first cup is the strongest, the third is the blessing. Our ceremony takes about an hour, because that is how long good talk takes.

We hold it twice a day in the corner by the window. Anyone can join a round for the price of a cup, or you can reserve the whole ceremony for your own guests.
