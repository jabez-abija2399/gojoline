Questions

Before you start

Do I need a computer or a till?

No. Any Android phone or a browser is enough. Printers and scanners are optional.

What happens when the internet is down?

You keep selling. Lemlem saves everything on the phone and sends it when the connection comes back.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by mobile money or bank transfer. You can stop at any time.

Can I bring my existing stock list?

Yes. Send us your Excel sheet or a photo of your notebook and we enter it for you.
