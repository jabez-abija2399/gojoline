What we arrange

Services for you and your family

  1. 01

    Baptism

    Boys are baptised on the 40th day and girls on the 80th, after the Sunday Liturgy. Bring the child's godparent and register at the office in the week before. Adults are baptised after a course of teaching.

  2. 02

    Holy matrimony

    For Orthodox couples, prepared with their father confessors. Please come to the office at least one month before the date.

  3. 03

    Confession and counsel

    Priests are in the compound after every Liturgy. We can also introduce you to a father confessor.

  4. 04

    Funeral and memorial prayers

    Funeral prayers on the day, and memorials on the third and fortieth days and at one year. Call us at any hour.

  5. 05

    Prayer for the sick

    Holy water in the compound each morning, and a priest to visit and pray at home or in hospital.

  6. 06

    House blessing

    A priest comes to bless a new home or business with prayer and holy water.

Father confessor

Someone to walk with you

In the Orthodox tradition every believer has a father confessor: a priest who knows your family, hears your confession and prepares you for Holy Communion.

If you do not have one yet, or have moved to Gerji from elsewhere, speak to the office. We will introduce you to one of our priests.
