Classes

A class for every age

  1. 01

    Children, ages 4 to 12

    Daily prayers, first hymns and Bible stories, with songs and drawing.

  2. 02

    Teenagers, ages 13 to 17

    The teachings of the faith, Church history and honest answers to the questions teenagers ask.

  3. 03

    Young adults, 18 and over

    Bible study, the lives of the saints and service in the parish: ushering, charity and teaching the little ones.

  4. 04

    Choir

    Hymns with kebero and tsenatsil. Rehearsals on Saturday afternoons; the choir sings at every feast.

  5. 05

    Traditional school (Abinet)

    Ge'ez reading, the Psalms and Wudase Mariam, learned by heart with the parish teacher on weekday afternoons.

  6. 06

    Summer course

    Eight weeks of morning lessons in July and August, ending with a hymn day for parents.

For parents

Your children are in good hands

Every class has two teachers, and the gate is watched until the last child is collected. We keep a register and call you if your child is absent two Sundays in a row.

Parents are welcome to sit in on a lesson at any time.

  • White clothes and a netela on Sundays
  • Bring a notebook; hymn books are provided
  • No fee, ever

Register a student

Register yourself or your child. We will call to tell you which class to join this Sunday.
