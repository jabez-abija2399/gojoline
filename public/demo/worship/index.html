Welcome

A parish that feels like family

Debre Tesfa St. Mary has served the families of Gerji for three generations. Here children learn their first hymns, couples are crowned, and the departed are remembered in prayer.

If this is your first time, or your first time in many years, come as you are. A deacon or a Sunday school member at the gate will gladly show you where to stand.

  • The Liturgy is in Ge'ez and Amharic
  • Children are welcome at every service
  • Priests are available for confession and counsel after the Liturgy

Coming up

Feasts and fasts ahead

The next dates in the Church calendar, and how we keep them here.

  • Hidar TsionNov 30Our annual feast. Vigil from 9:00 pm the night before; the Tabot goes out after the Liturgy.
  • Nativity (Genna)Jan 7Night Liturgy from 10:00 pm on the eve. The fast of the Prophets ends.
  • Epiphany (Timkat)Jan 19The Tabot leaves for the baptismal pool on the eve (Ketera) at 2:00 pm.
  • Asteryo MariamJan 29The feast of the Dormition of St. Mary. Liturgy at dawn.

The fast of the Prophets (Advent) runs from 24 November to the eve of the Nativity.

Giving

Give to the work of the Church

"God loves a cheerful giver." Choose where your gift goes. Give at the parish office after any service, or ask us for the bank and Telebirr details. Vows, candles, incense and raisins for the altar are received at the gate office.

  1. 01

    Tithes and offerings

    Keeps the daily services going: the clergy, incense, candles and the upkeep of the church.

  2. 02

    Building fund

    For the new Sunday school hall and the repair of the compound wall.

  3. 03

    Works of mercy

    Feeds elderly neighbours each week and helps children stay in school.
