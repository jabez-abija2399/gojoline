Your first visit

Come as you are. We will show you the rest.

You do not need to know the prayers to stand with us. Arrive a little before the Liturgy, and anyone in a white netela at the gate will be happy to help.

Holy Communion is received by baptised Orthodox Christians who have prepared with their father confessor. Everyone else is welcome to pray, listen and receive the blessing at the end.

  • Shoes are left at the door of the church
  • Women cover their hair; a netela is ideal, and we can lend one
  • Men stand on the left, women on the right
  • Benches along the walls for elders and anyone who needs to sit

Questions people ask

I am not Orthodox. May I come?

Yes. You are welcome at every service and at the evening teaching, which is a good place to start.

How long is the Sunday Liturgy?

About three hours, from 6:00 to 9:00 am. Many people then stay for the sermon and for senbete.

Is there parking?

There is space for about thirty cars inside the compound. On feast days it fills early, so a taxi is easier.

May I take photos?

In the compound, yes. Inside the church please ask a deacon first, and never during the Liturgy.
