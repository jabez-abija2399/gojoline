Before you book

How early should we book?

For January and April weddings, six months ahead. For other months, three is usually enough.

How do we pay?

30% holds the date, 40% one month before and the rest in the week of the event. Bank transfer or Telebirr.

Can we bring our own caterer or DJ?

Yes. We coordinate with any supplier you choose.

Do you work outside Addis Ababa?

Often in Bishoftu and Hawassa. Elsewhere, transport and rooms for the team are added to the quote.

What if the date changes?

We move your booking once, free, if the new date is open and you tell us 60 days ahead.

Is your date still free?

We take only a few events each month. Ask early.
