The salon

A calm room above a busy street

Saba opened with two chairs and one rule: nobody leaves with a sore scalp or a surprise bill. Today there are eight chairs, a nail table and a barber corner with its own door.

We use clean tools for every client, gentle products for natural hair, and we always show you the mirror before you pay.
