Questions we hear every day

Do I need an appointment?

Not for a wash, a cut or nails if a chair is free. For braids, colour and bridal work, please book at least a day ahead.

Should I bring my own extensions?

You can. We also sell braiding hair at the salon; the price is separate from the braiding.

Can you come to my home for a wedding?

Yes, inside Addis Ababa, for bridal bookings. Tell us the date and the number of people.

How do I pay?

At the salon, in cash, by Telebirr or by bank transfer.
