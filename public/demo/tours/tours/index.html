Sample itinerary

The historic north, day by day

  • Day 1Addis Ababa to Bahir DarMorning flight, boat to the Zege monasteries, sunset on Lake Tana
  • Day 2Blue Nile Falls, drive to GondarA one-hour walk to the falls; three hours by road
  • Day 3GondarRoyal enclosure, Fasilides' bath and Debre Birhan Selassie
  • Day 4Simien MountainsA day walk from Sankaber among the geladas, night in a lodge
  • Day 5Fly to LalibelaThe northern group of churches in the afternoon
  • Day 6LalibelaDawn at Bete Giyorgis, the southern group, a coffee ceremony in a family home
  • Day 7Asheten MaryamBy mule or on foot to the mountain monastery, a free afternoon
  • Day 8Back to Addis AbabaMorning flight, farewell dinner with music

Any day can be changed. Ask us for the day-by-day plan of the other trips.

In the price

What is included

Not included: domestic and international flights, lunch and dinner in towns, visa, tips and travel insurance.

  • Guide and scouts

    A licensed guide throughout, plus local guides and park scouts.

  • Transport

    4x4 or minibus with driver and fuel, and airport transfers.

  • Hotels and camps

    Clean mid-range hotels we have slept in ourselves; tents and mattresses on treks.

  • Meals

    Breakfast every day; all meals on treks and in the Danakil.

  • Entrance fees

    Churches, parks, museums and boat trips on the route.

  • Drinking water

    Bottled water in the vehicle every day.
