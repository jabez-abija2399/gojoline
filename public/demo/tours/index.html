Good to know

Before you travel

When is the best time to come?

October to March is dry and clear. The Simien is greenest in September and October; the Danakil is coolest from November to February.

Group or private?

Both. Group departures leave on fixed dates; a private trip can start any day for two or more.

How do I pay?

A 20% deposit by bank transfer or Telebirr, and the rest on arrival in Birr or US dollars.

How fit do I need to be?

Easy trips suit everyone. Moderate means 5 to 7 hours of walking a day at altitude. Hard means heat, rough roads and basic camps.
