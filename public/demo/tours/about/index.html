About us

Guides first, company second

Selam means peace, and it is how we greet every guest. We are guides and drivers who became a company, not the other way round.

We use family-run hotels, hire scouts and muleteers from the villages beside the trail, and keep groups small enough to be welcomed into a home for coffee.
