The story

Four hands, one loom, ten days

Every piece starts on a handloom in Shiro Meda, where two weavers work one length of cotton for up to ten days.

We cut it in our studio in Bole into shapes you can wear to work, to a wedding and on a Sunday afternoon, and we make each one only when you order it.
