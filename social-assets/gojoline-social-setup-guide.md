## 1. Telegram channel (5 minutes, do this first)

Telegram is where most people in Ethiopia will see you first.

1. Open Telegram → tap ✏️ (new message) → **New Channel**.
2. **Name:** Gojoline · **Description:** the About text · **Photo:** `profile-picture-1080.png`.
3. Choose **Public channel** → link: **t.me/gojoline** (or `gojoline_et`).
4. Skip adding members for now.
5. Settings → **Reactions**: turn on (lets people react with 👍 ❤️).
6. Settings → **Discussion**: optional. Create a group "Gojoline Chat" so people can comment and ask questions.

**First posts:** the vertical video with the Telegram text from `gojoline-posts.md`, then the full guide. Long-press the full guide → **Pin**.

## 2. Facebook Page (10 minutes)

1. Facebook app → ☰ Menu → **Pages** → **Create** (or go to facebook.com/pages/create).
2. **Page name:** Gojoline · **Category:** type "Website" or "Software Company" · **Bio:** the short bio.
3. **Profile picture:** `profile-picture-1080.png` · **Cover:** `facebook-cover-1640x624.png`.
4. Page settings → **Edit details**:
   - Website: https://gojoline.afridea.et
   - Email: your Gojoline Gmail
   - Username: **@gojoline**
5. **Add a button** → "Learn more" or "Sign up", pointing to https://gojoline.afridea.et.
6. Keep your personal profile as the **admin**; the Page posts under the Gojoline name.

## 3. Instagram (10 minutes)

1. Instagram app → your profile → tap your username at the top → **Add Instagram account** → **Create new account**.
2. Sign up with the Gojoline Gmail · username **gojoline** · same profile picture.
3. Edit profile: Name "Gojoline", bio = short bio, **Link** = https://gojoline.afridea.et.
4. ☰ → **Account type and tools** → **Switch to professional account** → **Business** → category "Website" or "Software".
5. Connect to the **Facebook Page** you just made (Instagram will offer it during the switch, or later in Accounts Center).
6. From now on, post Reels from **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com, or the app): one upload goes to both Facebook and Instagram.

## 5. YouTube channel (10 minutes)

1. Go to youtube.com and sign in with the **Gojoline Gmail**.
2. Profile picture → **Create a channel** → name **Gojoline** → handle **@gojoline**.
3. **Customize channel** (youtube.com/account → Your channel → Customize):
   - Picture: `profile-picture-1080.png` · Banner: `youtube-banner-2560x1440.png`
   - Description: the About text · Links: add https://gojoline.afridea.et as "Website"
4. **Verify the channel:** youtube.com/verify, with a phone number. This is free and needed to upload videos longer than 15 minutes and to add a **custom thumbnail**.
5. Upload the **full guide** (`gojoline-full-guide-16x9.mp4`):
   - Title, description with chapters, and tags: copy from `gojoline-posts.md`
   - Thumbnail: `gojoline-youtube-thumbnail.png`
   - Audience: **"No, it's not made for kids"**
   - Visibility: **Public**
6. Upload the **Short** (`gojoline-vertical-42s.mp4`). YouTube makes it a Short automatically because it's upright and under 3 minutes.
7. On the channel page → Customize → **Home tab** → add the full guide as the **featured video**.

## 6. LinkedIn (5 minutes, optional)

For now, just post from **your own profile**, using the LinkedIn text in `gojoline-posts.md`.
Later, if you want a company page: LinkedIn → **For Business** → **Create a Company Page** → Company, with the same name, logo, About text and website.

---

## Safety (5 minutes, important)

For **every** account:
1. Turn on **two-step verification** (Settings → Security). Use an authenticator app or SMS.
2. Save each account's **backup codes** somewhere safe (not in a screenshot in your gallery).
3. Use a **different password** for each account. A password manager helps.
4. On Facebook, add a second trusted person as Page **admin** so you don't lose the Page if your personal account is locked.
